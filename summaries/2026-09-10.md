### Hamiltonian fingerprints of a collective mode across the BCS-BEC crossover

**Authors:** Yogeshwar Prasad

- Tracks a layer‑odd collective mode in an attractive Hubbard bilayer from weak‑coupling hybridisation to the paired regime.
- Shows the mode’s Hamiltonian sensitivity shifts from interlayer tunnelling to intralayer hopping, evolving toward a \(1/U\) dependence.
- Uses sign‑free determinant QMC and Gaussian response theory to reveal interaction‑driven softening and a change from density‑to‑pair‑phase character.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09223)

### The energetics of force errors in machine-learned molecular dynamics

**Authors:** Peng Kang, Da Wan, Shulin Bai, Vincent Michaud-Rioux, Zhen Li, Yu Liu, Lei Zheng, Li-Dong Zhao

- Introduces a directional residual‑work coefficient that couples curvature mismatch and residual spatial distribution to predict work from force errors.
- Validates the coefficient on a 474‑atom Li‑electrolyte interface, achieving < 6 % deviation from measured work across 24 endpoints.
- Demonstrates that altering the initial velocity direction can reverse force‑work rankings, underscoring motion‑dependent energy transfer.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09251)

### Two-magnon response from light scattering in altermagnets

**Authors:** Shuyi Li, Lexu Zhao, Chunjing Jia

- Altermagnetism shifts the upper edge of the two‑magnon continuum to higher energy at X = (π,by an amount ∝ |δJ₂|, while leaving the zone‑center response unchanged.
- Quantum magnon–magnon interactions redistribute spectral weight to lower energies, producing a robust high‑energy reconstruction and a pronounced low‑energy two‑peak structure absent in linear spin‑wave theory.
- At K = (π/2,π/the interacting two‑magnon resonance is doubly degenerate in a conventional antiferromagnet, but finite altermagnetic exchange anisotropy lifts this degeneracy.

### Finite-Temperature Flat-Band Ferromagnetism in the Kagome Hubbard Model

**Authors:** Alon Strugatsky, Roser Valen\'i, Jonas B. Profe

- DMFT shows flat‑band ferromagnetism in the single‑orbital Kagome Hubbard model, matching exact/mean‑field predictions.
- Identifies a quasi‑ordered phase with a partially filled, spin‑polarized flat band pinned at the Fermi level, yielding persistent local spin fluctuations down to zero temperature.
- Demonstrates that these anomalous fluctuations arise intrinsically from flat‑band degeneracy, offering a minimal framework for correlated Kagome systems.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09289)

### Distinguishability Transitions from Global Quantum Snapshots

**Authors:** Catherine McCarthy, Sarang Gopalakrishnan, Romain Vasseur

- Generic time‑evolved states are locally featureless, yet global computational‑basis snapshots can separate them.
- Bayesian classifiers distinguish \(k\) random states with \(m\) shots, exhibiting a phase transition at \(x=m/\ln k\) with a derived critical value \(x_c\).
- The transition persists for low‑depth, noisy, and phase‑random circuits near anti‑concentration.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09296)

### Transient and universal regimes in quantum reaction-transport kinetics

**Authors:** Hossein Hosseinabadi, Roderich Moessner

- Fluctuations dominate low‑dimensional quantum annihilation ($D<2$), driving the system into a transport‑limited regime ($n\sim t^{-1/2}$) even for weak loss.
- At $D=2$ kinetics show logarithmic corrections; above $D_c=2$ mean‑field scaling is recovered, though mean‑field behavior can persist over long crossover times below $D_c$.
- Fluctuation‑induced effective elastic collisions redistribute momentum faster than losses, enabling a quasi‑stationary thermal state in $D>1$.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09305)

### Implicit size dependence of the valence electron concentration criterion in high-entropy alloys

**Authors:** Dennis Boakye, Chuang Deng

- VEC predicts FCC vs BCC in HEAs but ignores atomic size.
- Mixing enthalpy is almost size‑blind; size affects only the electron count through the covariance of \(V^{2/3}\) and valence.
- A size‑corrected VEC is just an affine rescaling of VEC and offers no better predictions; VEC already encodes size, failing only for large, electron‑rich elements.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09309)

**Authors:** Niladri Chakraborty, Clara C. Wanjura

- Directional amplification ↔ non‑Hermitian topology, now extended to quantum fluctuations.
- Phase‑preserving amplifiers: correlations grow exponentially with distance, diverging at the topological transition; entanglement stays local.
- Bosonic Kitaev chain: splits into two halves that are internally fully correlated yet mutually uncorrelated.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09312)

### Tuning Charge Density Wave transitions through lattice strain in NbSe3

**Authors:** Antoine Gallo Frantz, Aleksander Sinchenko, Luc Ortega, Pavel Grigoriev, Vladislav Kochev, Pierre Godard, Pierre Olivier Renault, Dominique Thiaudiere, David Le Bolloch, Vincent Jacques

- Tensile strain along or across NbSe3 chains changes lattice parameters, showing exotic Poisson ratios and anisotropic Young’s moduli.

- The differing strain responses of the two CDWs suggest band‑curvature modifications underlie the transport changes.

### Emergent Electronic Bond-Order Wave in a Quasi-One-Dimensional Chain

**Authors:** Honghao Wang, Tristan R. Cao, Pedro Schlottmann, Gang Cao

- Bond‑order wave (BOW) emerges in quasi‑1D Ba₉Rh₈O₂₄, with Rh–Rh bond inversion at 180 K.
- Transition shows heat‑capacity anomaly, dielectric loss suppression, and nonlinear, hysteretic I‑V with memristive switching.
- Demonstrates bond‑centered electronic order that overcomes 1D fluctuations, linking a dynamic bond liquid to a rigid, reconfigurable BOW.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09383)

### Orbital-selective electronic modes induced by doping and originating from distinct spin excitations: an essential feature of orbital-selective Mott transition

**Authors:** Masanori Kohno

- Doping a spin‑1 antiferromagnetic insulator in the Kanamori–Hubbard model generates orbital‑selective electronic modes inside the band gap.
- Conventional low‑energy spin excitations produce modes in the doped orbital, while inter‑orbital spin excitations produce modes in the undoped orbital, each with momentum‑shifted dispersion.
- These distinct, orbitally selective modes are identified as a key signature of the orbital‑selective Mott transition.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09741)

### Semi-Dirac States and Quantum Linear Magnetoresistance in Helimagnetic Pnictide MnP

**Authors:** Prasanta Chowdhury, Jyotirmoy Sau, Sanat Kumar Adhikari, Sourav Chowdhury, Peter Bencok, Matthias Gutmann, Souvik Chatterjee, Saurav Giri, Manoranjan Kumar, Subham Majumdar

- Large, non‑saturating linear magnetoresistance (~800 %) in MnP, especially in the field‑polarized FM2 state.
- Hall measurements reveal skew‑scattering dominated anomalous Hall at high T and a finite topological Hall in FAN and SCR phases.
- First‑principles show a semi‑Dirac‑like band at Y forming a tiny Fermi pocket that reaches the quantum‑limit, explaining the LPMR via Abrikosov theory.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09781)

### SIM-GRAPH: A universal guide to symmetric interactions

**Authors:** R. C. Verstraten, C. Morais Smith

- Introduces SIM‑GRAPH, a symmetry‑based graph reduction technique that projects interactions onto a symmetric subset, drastically reducing system size.
- Achieves exponential speedup over exact diagonalization for finite quantum systems with discrete symmetries.
- Provides two extensions enabling a tunable balance between computational cost and accuracy.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09996)

### Non-equilibrium dissipative stabilization of s- and d-wave superconductivity

**Authors:** Aleksey Lunkin, Yury Holubeu, Denis Gole\v{z}, Zala Lenar\v{c}i\v{c}

- Nonthermal pairing gap appears in BCS superconductors coupled to two baths, one at temperatures above equilibrium \(T_c\).
- The steady state is a generalized Gibbs ensemble with chemical potentials for Bogoliubov quasiparticles, breaking detailed balance.
- Strong, non‑perturbative stabilization of both \(s\)- and \(d\)-wave superconductivity, with prospects for cavity‑based experiments.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10062)
