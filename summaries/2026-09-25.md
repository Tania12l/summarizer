### Boundary and bulk perturbations in vectorial active matter

**Authors:** Giuseppe Fava

- Examines how global anisotropic environments break rotational symmetry in active matter and proposes ways to detect small anisotropies.
- Studies boundary‑induced symmetry breaking in confined polar active fluids, showing walls generate local anisotropies that penetrate far into the bulk.
- Demonstrates that boundary effects markedly alter both bulk and boundary dynamics in finite‑size flocking systems.

### Competing magnetic and spin vestigial orders from continuum field theory

**Authors:** Niccol\`o Francini, Lukas Janssen

- Developed a continuum field theory with independent vector and tensor fields to capture competing antiferromagnetic and spin‑nematic orders.
- RG analysis finds the multicritical fixed point at imaginary coupling, implying a first‑order triple point; a direct paramagnetic‑to‑AFM transition can be continuous, governed by cubic (3D) or Ising (2D) universality.
- The model predicts two‑step transitions with an intermediate spin‑vestigial (nematic) phase, controlled by the tensor mass and dimensionality.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28620)

### Stimulation of superconductivity in $d$-wave superconductors

**Authors:** Aidan Steineman, Maxim Dzero

- External EM radiation can enhance pairing in disordered \(d\)-wave superconductors via the Eliashberg effect, whereas a clean system shows no enhancement.
- The enhancement is confined to a finite frequency window set by gap, disorder, inelastic scattering, and temperature, with edges governed by quasiparticle redistribution, heating, and pair breaking.
- Unlike \(s\)-wave, the upper frequency threshold is not pinned at \(2\Delta\); it rises above the gap as the critical temperature is approached.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28624)

### Theory of extrinsic contributions to the full orbital current

**Authors:** James H Cullen, Dimitrie Culcer

- Developed a microscopic theory of extrinsic orbital Hall effect by solving the quantum‑kinetic equation for wave‑vector off‑diagonal density‑matrix elements, incorporating side‑jump and skew‑scattering within the non‑crossing approximation.
- Applied the theory to a massive Dirac cone and a particle‑hole‑asymmetric variant, finding disorder‑generated τ⁰ contributions comparable to the intrinsic current that cannot be isolated by disorder‑strength scaling.
- For the bare Dirac cone, the extrinsic correction cancels the conventional intrinsic value, leaving a total current exactly twice the quantum correction; particle‑hole asymmetry activates conventional skew scattering.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28628)

**Authors:** Ming-Rui Li, Zhengzhi Wu, Hong Yao

- Doping preserves PDW dominance via a protected Cooper logarithm; breaking time‑reversal while keeping nonsymmorphic symmetry yields a fully gapped topological spin‑triplet PDW with BdG Chern number

### Majorana zero modes in half-quantum vortices of pair density wave superconductors

**Authors:** Xinyu Sun, Hong Yao

- Spinless honeycomb model exhibits a PDW phase where half‑quantum vortices bind Majorana zero modes.
- Hartree‑Fock shows PDW relative‑phase stiffness ≈ superfluid stiffness near the PDW–Dirac transition, enabling vortex fractionalization and a field‑driven half‑vortex lattice transition.
- The resulting two‑flavor half‑vortex lattice hosts a Majorana lattice with geometry‑ and flux‑dependent bands, including Dirac nodes and zero‑energy Fermi lines.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28636)

### Interaction induced flattening of optical transition quantum geometry

**Authors:** Xu Yang, Justin C. W. Song

- Electron‑hole interactions flatten the quantum geometry of excitons, even when built from extended Bloch states.
- Localized particle‑hole excitations exhibit uniform, flat Hermitian curvature, whereas delocalized ones have non‑vanishing curvature.
- The resulting flat excitonic geometry quenches third‑order circular photoconductivity and ties the Hall response to that of the ground state in non‑magnetic systems.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28642)

### Bayesian Monitoring of a Diffusive Particle in One Dimension

**Authors:** Federico Gerbino, Guido Giachetti, Pierre Le Doussal, Andrea De Luca

- Bayesian tracking of a 1‑D diffusing particle with noisy continuous measurements yields a bounded Shannon entropy for constant monitoring, quantified via KPZ exact results.
- When monitoring intensity decays as \(t^{-\alpha}\), entropy grows logarithmically: \(\overline{S(t)}\sim \tfrac12\ln t\) for \(\alpha>1/2\) and \(\overline{S(t)}\sim \alpha\ln t\) for \(0<\alpha<1/2\).
- These regimes are derived through a replica mapping to a time‑dependent Lieb‑Liniger Hamiltonian, confirmed by simulations of discrete polymer models.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28655)

### Altermagnetic Kondo Logic via Floquet Symmetry Conversion

**Authors:** Haojie Shen, Xinchen Zhou, Baigeng Wang, Rui Wang

- Floquet symmetry conversion with an in‑plane AC field exposes hidden altermagnetic spin splitting, creating a local spin‑dependent hybridization channel.
- The resulting Kondo resonance splits with a crystalline form factor identical to the altermagnet, enabling Kondo tomography of the spin texture.
- The Kondo splitting is tunable by field orientation, providing a symmetry‑controlled route to Kondo‑based logic gates.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28680)

### Exact selection of a toric-code vison crystal in a flux-conditioned Kitaev model

**Authors:** Jiucai Wang, Chuan Chen

- Exactly solvable Kitaev extension where conserved ℤ₂ fluxes dictate both signs and connectivity of Majorana hopping.
- At a tuned loop point the matter Hamiltonian fragments into independent Majorana rings, yielding a frustrated Ising term plus a non‑negative residual that selects 2/3‑vison crystal ground states.
- The crystal ground space maps to a sheared square‑lattice toric code, giving fourfold topological degeneracy and demonstrating exact gauge‑matter feedback.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29004)

### Exact phase diagram of the XXZ Heisenberg chain in a staggered magnetic field

**Authors:** H. P. Zhang, Z. Song

- Exact analytical relation between exchange anisotropy and staggered field yields a highly degenerate ground‑state manifold with off‑diagonal long‑range order on the phase boundary.
- The boundary separates a ferromagnetic phase from a gapless Luttinger liquid, with a first‑order transition confirmed by DMRG.
- Reducing anisotropy further drives the gapless phase into a gapped quantum‑disordered phase, completing the full phase diagram.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29728)
