### Quantum-magneto oscillations in a supramolecular Mn(II)-[3 x 3] grid

**Authors:** O. Waldmann, S. Carretta, P. Santini, R. Koch, A. G. M. Jansen, G. Amoretti, R. Caciuffo, L. Zhao, L. K. Thompson

- High‑field torque magnetometry on Mn(II)-[3 × 3] grid reveals oscillatory torque vs. field above 5 T.
- Oscillations observed at 3 He temperatures, indicating quantum‑magneto effects.
- A theoretical model successfully reproduces the magneto‑oscillation behavior.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401304v1)

### Ferromagnetism in Fe-doped SnO2 thin films

**Authors:** J. M. D. Coey, A. P. Douvalis, C. B. Fitzgerald, M. Venkatesan

- Fe‑doped SnO₂ thin films are transparent ferromagnets with TC ≈ 610 K and Ms ≈ 2 Am²/kg.
- Mossbauer spectra reveal high‑spin Fe³⁺; only ~23 % of Fe ions are magnetically ordered, each carrying ~8 µB.
- Ferromagnetism attributed to Fe³⁺ coupling through an electron trapped in an oxygen vacancy (F‑center).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401293v1)

### Ultrafast Conductivity Dynamics in Pentacene Probed using Terahertz Spectroscopy

**Authors:** V. K. Thorsmolle, R. D. Averitt, X. Chi, D. J. Hilton, D. L. Smith, A. P. Ramirez, A. J. Taylor

- Measured picosecond photoconductivity in pentacene crystals via optical‑pump THz‑probe.
- Mobility drops from ~4 cm²/Vs at 30 K to ~2 cm²/Vs at room temperature.
- THz signal indicates rapid (< ps) trapping of photoexcited carriers, likely by excitons, polarons, or impurities.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401296v1)

### Phases of a rotating Bose-Einstein condensate with anharmonic confinement

**Authors:** A. D. Jackson, G. M. Kavoulakis, E. Lundh

- Identified three phases (multiple, single, mixed quantization) in a rotating BEC with quadratic‑plus‑quartic confinement.
- Derived a universal phase diagram; continuous transitions exact for weak coupling and small anharmonicity.
- Variational predictions agree with Gross‑Pitaevskii numerical solutions.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401292v1)

### Observation of narrow fluorescence from doubly driven four-level atoms at room temperature

**Authors:** Uday Kumar Khan, Jimmy Sebastian, N. Kamaraju, Andal Narayanan, R. Srinivasan, Hema Ramachandran

- Narrow fluorescence peaks appear in Rb‑85 under dual‑laser 3D molasses; one laser fixed detuned from cooling transition, the other scanned over repumping lines.
- Peaks form symmetric pairs whose separation increases with cooling‑laser detuning; large detunings yield additional small peaks.
- A simple model explains the observed spectral features.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401280v1)

### Origin of asymmetric reversal modes in ferromagnetic/antiferromagnetic multilayers

**Authors:** B. Beckmann, U. Nowak, K. D. Usadel

- Asymmetric reversal modes appear in certain exchange‑bias multilayers.
- Domain‑state simulations show the effect depends on the angle between the AFM easy axis and the applied field.
- At specific angles the FM reverses symmetrically via coherent rotation; at others it reverses asymmetrically, sometimes producing zero perpendicular magnetization.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401232v1)

### Vortex waves and the onset of turbulence in $^3$He-B

**Authors:** K. L. Henderson, C. F. Barenghi

- Finne et al. identified the intrinsic turbulence onset criterion \(q=\alpha/(1-\alpha')<1\) in \(^{3}\)He‑B.
- The criterion corresponds to marginally damped Kelvin waves; for \(q>1\) waves cannot grow to trigger reconnections.
- Analysis of rotating‑superfluid modes confirms \(q=1\) as the crossover between damped and propagating Kelvin waves in the long‑wavelength limit.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401212v1)

### Onsager Algebras and Lattice Fermions

**Authors:** Ioanna Kourkoulou, David Tong

- Exact Onsager algebra of free lattice fermions (staggered, Wilson, overlap) in 4D Hamiltonian/Euclidean settings.
- Reduces to full \(U(2N)\) chiral symmetry for \(N\) massless Dirac fermions in the continuum.
- Trade‑off between generator locality and maximal compact subgroup; quantisation varies by formulation.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31653v1)

### Occupancy Driven Phase Transitions in Gapped Systems

**Authors:** Ian Madden, Alex Kociubinski, Wouter Montfrooij

- Thermal excitations in gapped systems can shrink the gap; beyond a critical occupancy the system becomes unstable.
- The transition shows a divergent specific heat without an order parameter or full gap closure.
- Demonstrated with a two‑level model, superfluid ⁴He, and the hidden‑order transition in URu₂Si₂.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31564v1)

### Sign-problem-free Bosonic Auxiliary Field Quantum Monte Carlo

**Authors:** Yin-Kai Yu, Zhong-Chao Wei, Zi-Xiang Li

- Developed bosonic auxiliary‑field QMC (BAFQMC) that eliminates the sign problem.
- Proved time‑reversal symmetry and reflection positivity as sufficient conditions for sign‑problem‑free simulations, even on frustrated lattices.
- Benchmarked on a two‑flavor Bose‑Hubbard model on a triangular lattice, achieving quantitative agreement with exact diagonalization.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31541v1)

### Interaction-enhanced antiferromagnetic correlations in a Kondo ladder with a gapped band structure

- Narrow indirect band gap suppresses conventional RKKY, weakening spin correlations.
- Moderate on‑site Coulomb interaction boosts antiferromagnetic correlations and edge‑induced staggered magnetization.
- Interaction‑driven magnetic instability occurs regardless of Kondo coupling sign.

### Fit-error corrected auxiliary master equation approach for low-temperature nonequilibrium dynamical mean-field theory

**Authors:** Sebastian Alois Fuchs, Enrico Arrigoni, Tommaso Maria Mazzocchi

- Corrects AMEA’s low‑temperature fit error by scaling the auxiliary self‑energy with the ratio of physical/auxiliary self‑energies.
- Uses an approximate solver (IPT) to compute this ratio, yielding exact correction when either the fit or solver is exact.
- Benchmarks on spin‑polarized Anderson impurity and weakly coupled Hubbard layer, demonstrating improved DMFT accuracy.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30846v1)

### Impact of localized $4f$ magnetism on Berry curvature and the intrinsic anomalous Hall effect in CeRu$_2$Si$_2$

**Authors:** Takehiro Kobayashi, Shunsuke Hirai, Ibuki Terada, Michi-To Suzuki

- Intrinsic AHC stays finite in CeRu₂Si₂ even when Ce 4f states are localized and absent from the Fermi surface.
- AHC magnitude depends on the occupied Ce 4f state, as exchange splitting of conduction bands is induced.
- Localized 4f magnetism creates Berry curvature near the Fermi surface, allowing nonmagnetic conduction bands to contribute to a large intrinsic AHE.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30684v1)
