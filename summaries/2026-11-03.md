### Ultrafast X-ray-induced dephasing of valence electrons

**Authors:** J. Vonka, B. Pedrini, J. Ravnik, A. Steppke, E. Prat, S. Reiche, D. G. Mazzone, P. Anand, S. Allenspach, Y. Ekinci, U. Staub, S. -W. Huang, M. Sander, R. Mankowsky, H. T. Lemke, G. Aeppli, S. Gerber

- Valence electrons in silicon dephase within <20 fs under ultrafast X‑ray pulses, even when core ionization is minimal.
- Broadband, chirped X‑ray pulses provide few‑fs time encoding, showing outer‑electron dynamics outrun atomic core motion.
- Crystallographic studies relying on valence contributions require pulses shorter than ~50 fs, enabling separation of valence and lattice dynamics.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03635v1)

### The Virasoro Bootstrap: from the $c\sim1$ desert to coupled Potts

**Authors:** Stefanos R. Kousvos, Alessandro Piazza, Alessandro Vichi

- Developed a 2D bootstrap for \(c>1\) using Virasoro blocks via Zamolodchikov recursion and optimal polynomial interpolation.
- Isolated islands for three decoupled Ising models and a peninsula containing the coupled 3‑state Potts fixed point using mixed correlators.
- Found that single‑correlator bounds collapse just above \(c=1\), ruling out theories without a lightest scalar in their OPE and supporting the absence of compact CFTs in that region.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03565v1)

### An Intrinsically Gapless Topological Phase with Dipole Symmetry

**Authors:** Amogh Anakru, Linhao Li, Zhen Bi

- Boundary fractionalization in a dipole‑conserving 1D fermion model forces bulk gaplessness, creating an intrinsically gapless SPT (igSPT).
- The phase hosts a dipolar Luttinger liquid with half‑quantized boundary charges; edge configurations stay exactly degenerate at finite size due to dipole and particle‑hole symmetry.
- Dipole‑resolved entanglement spectra exhibit shifted conformal towers, indicating distinct conformal boundary conditions, confirmed by solvable limits and DMRG.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03538v1)

### Theory of the Ferroaxial Charge Density Wave in 1T-TiSe$_2$

**Authors:** Ilaria Maccari, Rafael M. Fernandes, Fernando de Juan, Maria N. Gastiasoro

- Proposes a secondary \(L_2^-\) CDW order to account for ferroaxial and nematic transitions in 1T‑TiSe₂.
- Landau theory with strain reproduces elastocaloric phase boundaries and ferroaxial symmetry breaking.
- Predicts a third threefold‑rotational symmetry breaking transition, matching the diverging nematic susceptibility.

### Symmetry breaking from conformality loss on holographic Rényi defects

**Authors:** Siqing Yu

- Rényi defect loses conformality at a critical index \(n_*\) when a charged operator merges with its alternate‑quantization partner.
- For \(n>n_*\) the defect flows to a source‑free, symmetry‑breaking conformal defect; for \(n<n_*\) a symmetry‑breaking source is required, making the breaking spontaneous at large \(N\).
- The mechanism is explicitly realized in the probe Abelian‑Higgs model on AdS\(_5\) (Bogomolny point), with unique, source‑free flows on large spheres and a scalar mass window that preserves the merger under backreaction.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03413v1)

### From photons to fractons: Casimir effect in higher-rank electrodynamics and its fractonic branch

**Authors:** Nicola Maggiore

- Three stable massless modes (2 TT tensors + 1 scalar) remain after imposing the fractonic Gauss constraint, giving a Casimir energy 3/2 that of Maxwell for ideal reflectors.
- The full Lorentz‑covariant theory contains an unstable vector sector with an unbounded Hamiltonian, so its boundary determinants do not describe a stable vacuum.
- The analysis isolates how the fractonic restriction removes the unstable sector and preserves only the physical radiative spectrum.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03269v1)

### Spin SYK-dot chains: entanglement bound and resource estimates towards a quantum advantage

**Authors:** Sayantan Dey, Abel Carreras, Siddhartha Patra

- Entanglement in spin SYK‑dot chains is bounded by ⌊p/2⌋ ln 2 plus inter‑dot terms, limiting tensor‑network and quantum simulation efficiency.
- Quantum advantage appears only for dots ≈15–21 qubits, demanding two‑qubit error rates ≈10⁻⁶, beyond NISQ reach.
- MPS and variational circuit tests confirm the entanglement bound, energy convergence, and fidelity decay predictions.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03207v1)

### Enforced Gaplessness by Combining Non-Anomalous Symmetries

**Authors:** Takamasa Ando, Kantaro Ohmori

- Symmetry spans of two embeddings of a common symmetry into larger non‑anomalous groups can enforce gaplessness.
- A 1+1D lattice model with a finite‑dimensional local Hilbert space (finite group ↔ U(²) is gapless for all parameter values.
- The paper also presents a 2+1D example and discusses spans involving only finite symmetries.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03164v1)

### Hidden variable $\mathbb{Z}_2$ topological order in a bilayer kagomé antiferromagnet

**Authors:** Hao Tian, Pengwei Zhao, Gang v. Chen

- Hidden $\mathbb{Z}_2$ topological order appears in a bilayer kagomé antiferromagnet via pseudospin-$1/2$ variables $\tau$ (topological) and $\mu$ (trivial).
- Physical spin signals mix fractionalized anyon continua with ordinary magnons, obscuring the topological order except in symmetry‑selected probes.
- The longitudinal intralayer response uniquely reveals the bare two‑vison continuum, offering a clear experimental fingerprint.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03073v1)

### Superinsulation, electric confinement in condensed matter

**Authors:** M. Cristina Diamantini, Carlo A. Trugenberger

- Superinsulation is a quantum phase dual to superconductivity, where U(electromagnetic fields become confining.
- In granular superconductors, tensionless XY vortices liberate magnetic monopoles that condense, forming magnetic flux tubes that confine electric fields into linear potentials between Cooper pairs.
- The resulting infinite resistance persists until a finite deconfinement temperature, with experiments confirming the linear confining potential and challenging phase‑only/disorder models.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02841v1)
