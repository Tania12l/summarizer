**Authors:** Rivo Herivola Manjakamanana Ravelonjato, Jean Patrice Rakotoniaina, Ravo Tokiniaina Ranaivoson, Wilfrid Chrysante Solofoarisina

- Extends Shockley‑Queisser limit to nanostructured cells using quantum phase‑space formalism, linking geometry to thermodynamics via a momentum‑variance matrix.

### Interferometric Signatures of Zero Modes in Fractional Quantum Hall-Superconductor Heterostructures

**Authors:** Junyi Cao, Ramanjit Sohal, Angela Kou, Eduardo Fradkin

- Parafermion zero modes in FQH–SC heterostructures cause stochastic switching in the fractional Josephson spectrum of a junction with a QPC.
- A two‑QPC Fabry‑Pérot interferometer shows fusion‑channel‑dependent interference; weak tunneling preserves the signal, strong tunneling suppresses it to zero.
- The vanishing interference in the strong‑tunneling limit reveals the non‑Abelian nature of the defects, even when the parent FQH state is Abelian.

### Layered matter that maintains spacing but loses stacking order

**Authors:** Osvaldo Trigueiro Neto, Paulo Henrique Michels-Brito, Bruno Ceccato Telli, Natalie Jasmin Eichstaedt, Leide P. Cavalcanti, Tom\'as S. Plivelic, Josef Breu, Jon Otto Fossum

- Swollen stiff, charged nanosheets keep a sharp, salinity‑tunable spacing while stacking order collapses to only 2–3 layers.
- A weak osmotic restoring slope (< 1 Pa nm⁻¹) holds the spacing, causing slow fault relaxation and a quenched, ageing‑like metastable registry.
- The effect is intrinsic (not defect‑driven) and should generalise to other stiff nanosheets such as clays, oxides, and graphene oxide.
