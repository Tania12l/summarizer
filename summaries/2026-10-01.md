### Accidental accuracy and formal consistency in $GW$+BSE: Exact benchmarks and regime-dependent error cancellation

**Authors:** Michael O. Atambo

- GW+BSE optical gaps appear accurate because an underestimated GW gap cancels an oversized bare‑exchange kernel, despite formal inconsistency.
- Benchmarking the extended Hubbard dimer shows this cancellation is optimal only in the weak‑binding regime; the frozen‑W construction fails at all binding strengths.
- For strongly bound excitons, static kernels break down, indicating the need for beyond‑static‑kernel physics.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38247)

### Hidden Angular Momentum Loop Currents in Symmetric Crystals

**Authors:** Rule Yi, Vincent P. Flynn, Benedetta Flebus

- Loop currents emerge from lattice dynamics, sustained by quantum and thermal fluctuations.
- In a symmetric 2‑D elastic lattice, neighboring plaquettes carry opposite currents while site angular momentum averages to zero.
- Directional elastic interactions in locally symmetry‑lowered environments organize these currents into a compensated, temperature‑persistent pattern.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38317)

### Morphodynamics of lipid vesicles under osmotic forcing

**Authors:** Nicholas Broussinos, David Saintillan

- Osmotic shock induces rapid buckling, wrinkle smoothing, then slow invagination of lipid vesicles, controlled by a single dimensionless parameter Γ.
- Linear stability predicts buckling wavelength and growth rate; buckling time scales as Γ⁻², matching simulations.
- Folding dynamics reverse elastic energy flow, with bending energy released and dissipated by membrane viscosity; folding time scales as Γ⁻²⁄³, confirming the continuum model.

### Sign problem and criticality in world-line quantum Monte Carlo methods

**Authors:** Rubem Mondaini, Richard T. Scalettar

- World‑line QMC exhibits a sign problem for free fermions, tied to non‑analytic behavior of a hard‑core boson reference model.
- The sign’s temperature/size dependence reflects the 2D Kosterlitz–Thouless transition of the boson system.
- Phase‑transition information remains encoded in the sign statistics even when direct sign sampling becomes impractical.
