### Reproducible capillary fluctuation analysis of solid-liquid interfaces for stiffness and anisotropy calculations

**Authors:** Kai Liu, Douglas E. Spearot, Damien Tourret

- Developed a diagnostics‑driven workflow for reproducible capillary‑fluctuation analysis on solid–liquid interfaces (Al example).
- Showed that linear spectra alone are insufficient; reliable stiffness and anisotropy need fitting windows matched to sampling, interface‑identification sensitivity, replica variability, and thickness convergence.
- Proposed a coexistence‑temperature‑based thickness rule that controls finite‑size effects while keeping ribbon geometries computationally efficient.

### Exact quantum spin liquids with topological order on maple-leaf and trellis lattices

**Authors:** Li Ern Chern, Roderich Moessner, Claudio Castelnovo

- Built exactly solvable spin models on maple‑leaf and trellis lattices that realize Z₂ quantum spin liquids with bond‑dependent anisotropic interactions.
- Determined low‑temperature flux ordering, mapped a topological phase diagram featuring Z₂ and Ising orders, and derived a Majorana mass term whose vanishing signals phase transitions.
- Linked vortex excitations to two anyon species in the dimer limit, showing smooth connectivity between distinct dimer assignments in the vortex‑free sector but fermion‑gap‑closing separations in the vortex‑full sector.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04319)

### Quantum oscillations of helical edge states of periodically deformed 2D topological insulator in magnetic field

**Authors:** A. V. Tsvetkova, P. D. Grigoriev, Ya. I. Rodionov

- Zeeman‑induced backscattering in a periodically deformed 2D TI edge produces oscillating forbidden‑band widths.
- In strong fields gaps close at discrete B; in weak fields semiclassical scattering is governed by complex‑infinity turning points.
- Edge conductance oscillates with B (not 1/B), period set by Fermi velocity and effective g‑factor, confirmed by theory and numerics.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04359)

### Fukui-Kawakami chains: spectrum and hidden $\mathfrak{gl}(1|1)$-symmetry

**Authors:** Rob Klabbers, Antoine Lefebvre

- FK chains are twisted deformations of the trigonometric Haldane–Shastry model; their spectra map to the untwisted HS chain via a twist parameter.
- A subset of states obey twisted Bethe equations and Yangian highest‑weight motifs, while other descendants have energies as sums of two single‑particle terms, indicating hidden symmetries.
- With antiperiodic boundary conditions the FK chain coincides with the minimally polarised long‑range model, revealing a hidden \(\mathfrak{gl}(1|\) symmetry

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04378)

### Composition-dependent nonlinear viscoelastic-viscoplastic behavior and constitutive framework for digitally mixed polymers spanning the glass transition

**Authors:** Beijun Shen, Mary C. Boyce

- Digitally mixed PolyJet elastomer/glass polymers transition continuously from elastomeric hysteresis to glassy yielding as Vero fraction rises.
- Dynamic mechanical analysis shows a single glass‑transition temperature that shifts with composition and frequency, enabling a time‑composition equivalence.
- A unified constitutive model—an equilibrium hyperelastic network plus three rate‑dependent branches—captures compression responses across all mixtures.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04399)

### Breakdown of Anomalous Hall Scaling in Dilute Kondo System

**Authors:** Arnab Bhattacharya, Prashant Singh, Ajay Kumar, Afsar Ahmed, Yaroslav Mudryk, I. Das, Anis Biswas

- Berry‑curvature driven anomalous Hall effect in Ru₂Mn₀.₅Ti₀.₅Ge, a dilute Kondo Heusler alloy with flat bands near the Fermi level.
- Hall response is non‑monotonic, violating Fermi‑liquid scaling near the magnetic transition and recovering only at low temperatures when Kondo coherence develops.
- Ab initio calculations show Berry curvature originates from Kondo‑hybridized flat bands and spin‑orbit‑coupled anticrossings, underscoring the interplay of topology and strong correlations.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05319)

### Eye-opening bounds on cusps

**Authors:** Ryan A. Lanzetta, Ian Moult, Yifan Wang

- Links smooth and fusion limits, providing bounds on defect‑changing operator dimensions, Casimir energies, and subleading fusion data.
