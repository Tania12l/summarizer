### A Liquid Film Can Survive without Being Bulk-Like: Rupture and Mechanical Isotropy Set Different Thickness Limits in Lennard-Jones Films

**Authors:** Jose Luis Altamirano-Corona, Alfonso Lemus-Solorio, Francisco Villanueva-Mejia, Mariana Ramos-Estrada, Jose L. Rivera

- Bulk-like mechanical isotropy requires a film thickness of ~11–15 σ, whereas rupture occurs at ~6–7 σ.
- Rupture is largely independent of interaction range, weakly size‑dependent, and occurs via activated events within ~10⁵ τ.
- The isotropy threshold increases with cutoff distance but is insensitive to temperature and lateral size; films between the two limits are long‑lived yet non‑bulk.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25008)

### Exact Fractional Chern Insulators from Hubbard Interactions

**Authors:** Jonah Herzog-Arbeitman, Rui-Heng Liu, Jiangping Hu, Chen Fang

- Exact Laughlin, Read‑Rezayi, and Halperin wavefunctions are shown to be zero‑energy ground states of Hubbard‑interaction parent Hamiltonians in tight‑binding Chern bands.
- A Landau‑dual multi‑orbital Chern band is constructed by sampling spin‑\(s\) Dirac Landau levels on a lattice, yielding analytic wavefunctions via the matrix coordinate \(X+iY+J\).
- The work proves that fractional Chern insulator ground states exist beyond ideal flat‑band conditions, extending the exact pair framework to realistic Hubbard models.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25010)

### Umklapp-enhanced high-$T_c$ topological superconductivity mediated by magnons in $p$-wave magnets

**Authors:** Kristoffer Leraand, Asle Sudb{\o}

- Magnon exchange in a p‑wave magnet produces nodal p‑wave superconductivity, with Tc rising steeply as the Fermi surface nears the magnetic Brillouin‑zone boundary.
- Umklapp scattering of small‑q magnons between opposite Fermi‑surface segments boosts pairing because electron‑magnon vertex cancellations across the magnetic unit cell are reduced.
- Momentum‑resolved winding numbers predict zero‑energy Majorana flat bands at symmetry‑preserving edges where bulk nodes project to distinct momenta.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25015)

### Chern Numbers from Symmetry-Enforced Zeros and Dispersion Shifts

**Authors:** Yoonseok Hwang, Henry Davenport, Frank Schindler

- Symmetry‑enforced wave‑function zeros and their vorticities, obtained from overlaps with reference states, encode Chern numbers.
- Band‑dispersion shifts ∝|overlap|² reveal zero positions and vorticities, allowing topology to be read from energy spectra alone.
- The framework applies to multicomponent/multiband systems, uses compact localized states as references, and yields a lower bound on the quantum metric at zeros.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25028)

### Anyon Coherent States in Fractional Chern Insulators

**Authors:** Pedro Fittipaldi de Castro, Haylen Gerhard, Wladimir A. Benalcazar

- Systematic operator construction of maximally‑localized anyon wavefunctions for FQH and fractional Chern insulators, even in non‑ideal bands.
- Coherent anyon states are zero‑mode annihilation operators that saturate projected‑density uncertainty, with Gaussian overlap decay set by the effective magnetic length.
- Their existence relies on the anyon band’s nontrivial topology; Bloch coefficient winding encodes the anyon band Chern number, permitting guiding centers anywhere on the lattice.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25034)

### Topological (in)equivalence encoded in zeros of wavefunction overlaps

**Authors:** Carolina Paiva, Bruno Mera, Roni Ilan, Raquel Queiroz

- Relative topology of two gapped band structures is encoded in the zeros of the overlap matrix between their occupied subspaces.
- In 2D class A the total winding charge of these zeros equals the Chern‑number difference; in class AII the parity of the half‑BZ charge yields the ℤ₂ invariant (Kane–Mele Pfaffian).
- Crystalline symmetries constrain zero positions and charges, as demonstrated in the Haldane, Kane–Mele, and Qi–Wu–Zhang models.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25037)

### Chiral Phonons Protected by Symmetry in a Strongly Anharmonic Helical Material

**Authors:** Supriyo Ghosh, Dipti Jasrasaria

- Rotational symmetry in crystalline Te imposes pseudo‑angular‑momentum selection rules that restrict phonon‑phonon mixing at symmetry‑invariant Brillouin‑zone points.
- These rules preserve the circular polarization (chirality) of vibrational modes up to 600 K, even as lifetimes sharply decrease.
- Angular‑momentum transport under a thermal gradient is governed by lifetimes, dropping ~100× from 10 K to 600 K.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25089)

### Mixtures of a fluid with competing interactions and a simple solvent near a hard wall

**Authors:** Ivan Kravtsiv, Oksana Patsahan, Taras Patsahan

- Mean‑field theory for a binary soft‑particle mixture with SALR and Yukawa interactions near a hard wall, deriving coupled density equations and a contact theorem.
- Linearized solution gives closed‑form density profiles whose amplitudes are fixed by boundary continuity, matching the full nonlinear results.
- Predicts temperature‑ and density‑driven microphase separation into alternating layers, in good agreement with molecular‑dynamics data.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25097)

### Current-driven resistive switching in high-field heavy fermion metal UTe2

**Authors:** Ling Zhang (Mark), Chunyu (Mark), Guo, David Graf, Carsten Putzke, Mitchell M. Bordelon, Eric D. Bauer, Sean M. Thomas, Filip Ronning, Priscila F. S. Rosa, Philip J. W. Moll

- Current densities >10 kA/cm² induce a sharp resistivity jump in UTe₂, creating a high‑resistivity state in both paramagnetic and field‑polarized regimes.
- The critical current density rises with temperature, ruling out Joule‑heating and pointing to an intrinsic electronic instability.
- The same current‑driven transition occurs on both sides of the metamagnetic transition, implying a common underlying physics between the low‑field paramagnetic and high‑field field‑polarized states.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25792)
