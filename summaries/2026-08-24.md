### Ultralow-Field Triplon Condensation in a Spin-Ladder Magnet

**Authors:** Ankit Labh, Ross H. Colman, Jakub Šebesta, Noah Oefele, Elsa Lhotel, Adam Berlie, Paul Steffens, Oksana Zaharko, Pascal Manuel, Iurii Kibalin, Philipp Gegenwart, Dominik Legut, Johanna K. Jochum, Alexander A. Tsirlin, Petr Čermák

- First ultralow‑field triplon BEC observed in a spin‑ladder, with μ₀H_c1 = 17 T in Henmilite.
- Thermodynamic, μSR, and neutron data show a quantum‑disordered coupled‑ladder ground state, refuting earlier zero‑field AF order.
- An asymmetric ordered dome (H_c2 ≈ 2 T) is reproduced by QMC, placing Henmilite just on the gapped side of the ladder‑ordering

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21316v1)

### Neural quantum states in condensed matter: advances, best practices, and prospects

**Authors:** Jonas B. Rigo, Björn J. Wurst, Rajah Nutakki, Markus Schmitt, Dante Kennes

- Neural quantum states combine neural-network parametrizations with Monte Carlo sampling to flexibly represent many‑body wave functions.
- Recent work applies them to frustrated magnets, lattice fermions, and non‑equilibrium dynamics, emphasizing symmetry constraints, optimization, and sampling strategies.
- Key challenges remain: learning sign/phase structures, reducing variational bias, scaling optimization, and stabilizing real‑time evolution.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21291v1)

### Two distinct gap structures in the mid-infrared optical conductivity of the Hubbard model

**Authors:** Dongwook Kim, Karsten Held

- Two distinct gaps—pseudogap (AF spin fluctuations) and Mott‑Hubbard—drive universal MIR conductivity.
- Each gap yields a characteristic MIR spectral shape and distinct doping dependence, enabling experimental separation.
- Doping evolution: low‑doping Mott peak in visible; high‑doping broad MIR response from waterfall‑induced correlations, matching experiments.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21284v1)

### Emergent Vibronic Spectral Hierarchy in a Kagome Flat-Band Insulator

**Authors:** Jun Shu, Jun Shen, Yanmin Zhang, Hong Du, Qingsong Wang, Zeyuan Wang, Bin Wang, Zeliang Xu, Dengjing Wang, Hengfu Lin, Zunming Lu, Lei Qin, Jie Yuan, Jinbo Peng, Zhida Song, Fedor V Kusmartsev, Anna Kusmartseva, Kui Jin, Ruidan Zhong, Ge He

- Raman spectra reveal a giant Franck‑Condon progression from a nearly dispersionless 7 cm⁻¹ phonon, with a 6 cm⁻¹ comb‑like fine structure.
- The comb spacing is field‑independent, yet its spectral weight is strongly tunable by magnetic field, indicating a coupled electron‑vibrational manifold.
- The 260–280 K transition is an electronic localization without conventional structural or magnetic order, implicating dispersionless phonons in vibronic self‑trapping and the insulating phase.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21250v1)

### Berry-Curvature Effect in Anomalous Transport

**Authors:** V. P. Mineev

- Derives Berry‑phase contribution to anomalous transport via quasiclassical matrix kinetic equation in altermagnets and noncentrosymmetric metals.
- Extends beyond anomalous Hall effect to include anomalous heat flow and thermoelectric currents driven by temperature or chemical‑potential gradients.
- Provides an alternative formulation to Di Xiao et al., emphasizing the role of a field‑dependent density of states.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21161v1)

### Pair size versus coherence length and quantum geometry in the spin-orbit coupled BCS-BEC crossover

**Authors:** R. N. Kalkan, M. Iskin

- Exact pair‑size tensor in helicity basis separates into intraband and quantum‑geometric interband parts set by the quantum metric.
- Two many‑body lengths: Cooper‑pair size (mean‑field) and coherence length (Gaussian fluctuations); they coincide in BCS but diverge across the crossover.
- In 3D Rashba/Weyl and 2D Rashba SOC, pair size monotonically decreases with SOC, while coherence length exhibits a pronounced minimum at the crossover, with quantum‑geometric terms contributing up to ~1/3 of the pair size.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21110v1)

### Doping-driven Mott transition from steady-state density functional theory

**Authors:** David Jacob, Stefan Kurth

- Applies steady‑state i‑DFT to the Hubbard model’s doping‑driven Mott transition.
- Links the i‑DFT exchange‑correlation bias to quasiparticle weight using Fermi‑liquid theory, guiding functional construction.
- Numerical results confirm i‑DFT captures the Mott transition.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21029v1)

### A Classification of Translation-Invariant Quantum Codes in Any Dimension

**Authors:** Andrew Li, Dominic J. Williamson

- Classifies D‑dimensional translation‑invariant quantum codes using length‑D chain complexes.
- Proves such codes are equivalent to multiple copies of the D‑dimensional toric code.
- Applies to multivariate multicycle codes where the number of variables equals the number of cycles.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20981v1)

### Quasi-one-dimensional topological band structure and van Hove singularities in monolayer TaIrTe$_4$ from laser $μ$-ARPES

**Authors:** Honey Boban, Tanguy Prongué, Amarjyoti Choudhury, Dario Marchiani, Andrés Bareño, Felix Eder, Enrico Giannini, Fabian O. von Rohr, Alberto F. Morpurgo, Marco Gibertini, Anna Tamai, Felix Baumberger

- μ‑ARPES reveals a highly anisotropic, quasi‑1D band structure in monolayer TaIrTe₄.
- A saddle‑point van Hove singularity exists, yet it lies at a carrier density where transport shows no anomalies.
- Quasiparticle linewidths indicate weak correlations, suggesting van Hove singularities and electron interactions play a secondary role in the quantum spin Hall phase.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20978v1)

### Spin models with critical ground space degeneracy from Lie algebra relations

**Authors:** Andras Molnar, Efekan Kökcü, Norbert Schuch, Bojko N. Bakalov

- SO(-symmetric spin chain built from an MPS of so(generators, encoding the algebra’s quadratic relations.
- Ground‑state degeneracy: OBC → quadratic (one irrep per odd dimension); PBC → linear (singlet + maximal‑spin ferromagnet), yielding gapless modes.
- Construction extends to SO(d) and other compact simple Lie groups, illustrating that small parent Hamiltonians of injective MPS can exhibit critical, non‑unique ground spaces.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20849v1)
