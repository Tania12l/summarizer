### Ferromagnetism in Fe-doped SnO2 thin films

**Authors:** J. M. D. Coey, A. P. Douvalis, C. B. Fitzgerald, M. Venkatesan

- Fe‑doped SnO₂ thin films are transparent ferromagnets with Tc ≈ 610 K and Ms ≈ 2 Am²/kg.
- Mossbauer reveals high‑spin Fe³⁺; only ~23 % of Fe ions are magnetically ordered, each contributing ~8 µB.
- Ferromagnetism attributed to Fe³⁺ coupling through an electron trapped in a bridging oxygen vacancy (F center).

### Vortex waves and the onset of turbulence in $^3$He-B

**Authors:** K. L. Henderson, C. F. Barenghi

- Finne et al. identified the intrinsic turbulence onset criterion \(q=\alpha/(1-\alpha')<1\) in \(^{3}\)He‑B.

**Authors:** Z. Z. Alisultanov, A. V. Kavokin

### Fractional Chern Insulator in a Finite-Range One-Band Model

**Authors:** Tong Liu, Hoi Chun Po

- Realizes a fractional Chern insulator in a single‑band, finite‑range interaction model on a square lattice without single‑particle quantum geometry.
- At 1/3 filling, exhibits a three‑fold ground‑state degeneracy, Hall conductance \(-2e^2/3h\), and fractional charge pumping (verified by ED and DMRG).
- Attributes the FCI phase to nontrivial topology arising in the repulsive two‑particle sector, offering a microscopic view of interaction‑driven fractional quantum Hall order.
