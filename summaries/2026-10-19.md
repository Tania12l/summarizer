### Universal link between structural relaxation and self-diffusion in simple supercooled liquids

**Authors:** Mingyu Zhu, Xinyu Chang, Licun Fu, Yiming Zheng, Lijin Wang

- Universal scaling links self‑diffusion and structural relaxation time at constant dynamical heterogeneity.
- Fractional Stokes‑Einstein exponent is invariant across liquids of different fragility.
- Scaling reproduces data for supercooled water and silica, underscoring dynamical heterogeneity’s central role.

### Noise-Induced Restoration of Entropy Concavity Near Gaussian Equilibrium

**Authors:** Fayez Abu-Ajamieh

- Demonstrates that adding Gaussian noise restores concavity of entropy for partially thermalized one‑dimensional states, deriving an exact endpoint‑curvature formula separating OU‑restoring and fourth‑cumulant terms.
- Extends the result to nonsmooth generalized‑Gaussian densities \(e^{-a|x|^p}\) (\(p>3/2\)) and proves an outer‑region theorem bounding endpoint obstructions to two near‑equilibrium sectors.
- Provides spectral and MMSE reformulations of the key inequality \(A\ge2\delta\), proving it near Gaussian equilibrium and sharpening bounds for the leading non‑Gaussian Hermite mode.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22386)

### Thermodynamics of coupled electromagnetic and mass fields

**Authors:** Fei Wang

- Unified Gibbs–Duhem relation incorporating kinetic free‑energy links free‑energy, chemical potentials, pressure, and transport momentum.
- Lagrangian framework unifies diffusion, momentum transport, and electromagnetic dynamics, reproducing Cahn–Hilliard/Korteweg coupling.
- Distinguishes dissipative vs nondissipative transport; isentropic propagation speed follows from pressure‑density derivative, yielding sound and light speeds.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22391)

### Anyon bound states in MoTe$_2$ Fractional Chern Insulators

**Authors:** Tianhong Lu, Luiz H. Santos

- Anyon–exciton bound states form between a mobile dipolar exciton and an \(e/3\) quasihole in twisted bilayer MoTe\(_2\) fractional Chern insulators.
- MeV‑scale binding and strong exciton–quasihole entanglement arise from exciton mobility and interlayer distance, with spatial correlations revealing a localized \(e/3\) depletion core and a moiré‑induced octupolar charge distribution.
- Interlayer tunneling anisotropy deforms the internal octupolar profile while preserving the total charge, linking the anyon’s internal structure to the underlying electronic quantum geometry.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22473)

### Fractional Chern insulators and composite Fermi liquids vs. charge order in twisted MoTe$_2$

**Authors:** Enrique Aguilar-M\'endez, Glenn Wagner

- Twisted MoTe₂ at ~7° hosts spin‑valley‑locked Chern bands with bandwidth < interaction scale, enabling Landau‑level‑like phases (QH, composite Fermi liquid, charge order).
- Exact diagonalization reveals charge‑ordered states at ν = −¼, −⅓, −½, −⅔, tunable by displacement field and twist angle.
- Band‑mixing beyond a single valley band is crucial to reconcile numerical phase diagrams with experimental observations.

### Attraction from repulsion in an interacting Chern band: chiral superconductivity, generalized Hall crystals, and competing fractional Chern insulators

**Authors:** Fabian Pichler, Clemens Kuhlenkamp, Ashvin Vishwanath, Michael Knap

- Tensor‑network study of repulsive spinless fermions on a checkerboard Chern band uncovers competing FCIs, chiral p‑wave superconductivity, and a Hall crystal.
- Enhancing band dispersion turns Jain‑sequence FCIs into chiral SC on both sides of half filling; at half filling strong coupling yields a doubled‑unit‑cell Hall crystal with quantized Hall conductance.
- Weak‑coupling RPA shows metallic fluctuations generate long‑range attraction, reproducing the SC pairing symmetry and selecting the Hall crystal via Hartree‑Fock.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22498)

### Bulk-Defect Correspondence in Algebraic Classical Spin Liquids

**Authors:** Rafael Flores-Calder\'on

- Pinch‑point singularities in momentum space carry a winding number that fixes the low‑energy structure of algebraic spin liquids.
- Nonmagnetic vacancies in 2D expose this invariant through real‑space winding of surrounding spin textures, proving a bulk‑defect correspondence.
- The correspondence is shown for rank‑\(n\) U(spin liquids and verified numerically on kagome, checkerboard, and honeycomb‑snowflake lattices, allowing vacancy probes of emergent gauge theories.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22585)

### Transport Signatures of a Kinetically Self-Trapped Nagaoka Polaron

**Authors:** Yusong Xiao, Yuan Wan

- Calculated dynamical mobility of a single hole in the square‑lattice $t$‑model via resummation‑based QMC and analytic continuation.
- Found a crossover from incoherent Drude‑like response to a finite‑frequency peak as temperature lowers, indicating vanishing dc mobility.
- Identified the peak as an internal mode of a hole confined by its ferromagnetic cloud, demonstrating transport as a probe of kinetic self‑trapping.

### Crystal Field-Phonon Coupling in ErFeO$_3$ Single Crystal Via Inelastic Neutron Scattering

**Authors:** Guochu Deng, Xiaoxuan Ma, Wanting Yang, Wei Ren, Shixun Cao, Zhenxiang Cheng, Garry J. McIntyre

- CF excitations (~5 meV, 12 meV) hybridize with phonons, shifting energies, redistributing intensity, and narrowing linewidths.
- Coupling strengthens from 300 K to 650 K, then slightly weakens at 750 K; thermally suppressed CF peaks re‑emerge through phonon interaction at specific Q.
- DFT phonon calculations confirm the CF‑phonon coupling, underscoring its major influence on the excitation spectra of rare‑earth orthoferrites.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23015)
