### Generating is not discovering: a pre-registered physics judge for AI-proposed superconductors, calibrated on six known superconductors and one negative control

**Authors:** Reinaldo In\'acio

- Created a pre‑registered physics judge that uses DFT+U, Wannier, RPA, gap equations, symmetry classification, and manufacturability to assess AI‑proposed superconductors, calibrated on six known materials.
- The judge correctly classifies all known superconductors and rejects a ruthenium analogue as non‑superconducting, validating its predictive accuracy.
- Applied the judge to audit 150 materials and generative model outputs, revealing that many AI‑generated candidates lack the essential physics for superconductivity.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10614)

### Lattice 2-group symmetries: operators, defects, and gauging

**Authors:** Lucas Z. Brito, Salvatore D. Pace

- Constructed lattice realizations of finite 2‑group symmetries in 2+1d quantum systems, distinguishing split (trivial Postnikov) and central (trivial action) cases.
- Built symmetry operators on the full Hilbert space: onsite for split 2‑groups, non‑onsite for central 2‑groups (made onsite with ancillae).
- Analyzed how the Postnikov class and group action affect operator transformations, defects, dual fusion 2‑category, and proposed solvable symmetric Hamiltonians exhibiting spontaneous 2‑group symmetry breaking.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10682)

### A Unified Theory of Collective Magnon and Orbiton Excitations in Altermagnets

**Authors:** Bishal Das, Chanchal K. Barman, Aftab Alam

- Unified Kugel'-Khomskii model on a decorated square lattice captures both inequivalent non‑magnetic environments and correlation‑driven orbital ordering in altermagnets.
- Self‑consistent spin‑wave/orbiton mean‑field analysis reveals unhybridized, interdependent magnon and orbiton modes with characteristic chiral splitting.

### Consensus in Effective Model Inference for Disordered Ising Systems

**Authors:** Ashveed Ashraf

- Consensus quantifies agreement among independently trained models on disordered Ising data; its width reflects reproducibility.
- The consensus variance separates into a finite‑sampling term (∝ 1/Fisher information) and a quenched‑disorder term; both reach minima near the pseudocritical temperature, sharpening with disorder.
- The two minima arise from distinct mechanisms: the sampling term inherits the critical Fisher‑information peak, while the disorder term’s critical minimum appears only at fourth order in disorder strength via covariance of linear and cubic responses.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10731)

### Competing Interlayer Loop Currents and Superconductivity in the Bilayer $t$-$J_\perp$-$V$ Model

**Authors:** Luciano Zinni, Fabricio G\'omez, Jun Zhan, Mat\'{\i}as Bejas, Xianxin Wu, Andreas P. Schnyder, Andr\'es Greco

- J⊥ drives both out‑of‑plane s‑wave superconductivity and a z‑axis bond‑order phase that produces loop currents breaking time‑reversal symmetry.
- Competition between loop‑current order and superconductivity yields a dome‑shaped Tc, peaking near the z‑BOP quantum critical point.
- Resulting phase diagram: pure loop‑current, low‑doping coexistence, pure superconducting at higher doping, and a correlated metallic state.

### Instabilities in self-consistent diagrammatic approaches and how to cure them

**Authors:** Marcel Gievers, Herbert E{\ss}l, Stefan Rohshap, Anna Kauch, Alessandro Toschi

- Self‑consistent diagrammatic schemes can converge to unphysical fixed points at intermediate‑to‑strong interactions.
- The problem stems from unstable eigenvalues of the iteration Jacobian, not from the diagrammatic theory itself.
- Inverting the unstable eigendirections of the Jacobian restores convergence to the physical fixed point across all parameter ranges.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11405)

### Robust Spin-1 Haldane Topology under Itinerant Doping

**Authors:** Satoshi Nishimoto

- Doped spin‑1 Haldane SPT retains string order, spin gap, and Haldane entanglement over a broad doping range.
- The low‑energy theory is a metallic C1S0 phase: one gapless charge mode with a gapped spin sector.
- At high doping a reentrant ferromagnetic phase appears, with FM boundaries governed by spin–charge factorization (weak Hund) and rung‑triplet binding (strong Hund).
