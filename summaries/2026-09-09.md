### Superdiffusive two-dimensional superconductors

**Authors:** Iogann Tolbatov, Luca Salasnich

- Introduces a non‑local field theory for 2D superconductors using a Riesz fractional Laplacian.
- Finds vortex‑antivortex interaction \(V(r)\propto r^{2-\alpha}\) (\(1<\alpha<2\)), suppressing thermal vortices and enabling true long‑range order at finite \(T\).
- Derives a fractional London equation with a power‑law kernel, linking to the anomalous Pippard limit and unifying non‑local electrodynamics.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05447)

### Tilt control of coverage heterogeneity for hard spherocylinders locked on a sphere

**Authors:** Jonathan Washburn, Hartmut L\"owen, Elshad Allahyarov

- Tilt angle tunes the width of the depletion zone around the poles; meridian locking yields the widest depletion, while tilting toward latitude shrinks it.
- Uniformity of the polar density is governed by geometry, not tilt: variance ∝(L²/(R D))^α with α≈1–3; long rods on small spheres cannot be made uniform.
- The variance‑minimizing tilt lies between ~32°–55°, with the golden‑ratio tilt (~36°) often near optimal; the density is equator‑heavy except at high coverage and tilt.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05486)

### Signatures of spin-wave dynamics in quasiparticle interference

**Authors:** Alireza Akbari, Peter Thalmeier

- QPI shows a crossover at the magnon‑emission edge: below it, real self‑energy shifts dominate; above it, imaginary part causes broadening.
- Static AF order reconstructs bands, while one‑magnon processes add a momentum‑ and frequency‑dependent self‑energy, producing bias‑asymmetric intensity.
- Fourier‑transform tunneling spectroscopy can distinguish static magnetic reconstruction from dynamical spin‑wave renormalization.

### Electronic correlations and fluctuating lattice distortions in vanadium dioxide

**Authors:** Antonio Picano, Martin Eckstein, Francesco Grandi

- Developed stochastic semiclassical DMFT that couples correlated electrons to fluctuating lattice modes via nonlinear electron‑phonon interactions.
- Applied to a two‑orbital VO₂ model, finding two separate melting temperatures for dimerization and shear distortions, yielding insulating, metallic, and high‑symmetry phases.
- Showed dimerization collapse is driven by electron correlations, while shear melting is governed by lattice entropy.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06707)
