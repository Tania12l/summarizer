### Localization Landscapes as Operator-Induced Geometry for Heterogeneous Systems

**Authors:** Josu\'e Garc\'ia-\'Avila (Department of Mechanical Engineering, Columbia University)

- Localization landscape \(u\) from a single source solve yields effective potential \(W_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/u\), exposing hidden barriers, wells, and weakly communicating compartments in heterogeneous operators.
- \(W_{\mathrm{eff}}\) defines an operator‑aware geometry (basin partitions, Agmon neighborhoods) that explains low‑energy eigenmode support, sharpens locality in attention‑like interactions, and modulates diffusion bottlenecks.
- Demonstrated on discrete Schrödinger‑type and heterogeneous reaction‑diffusion operators—including a finite‑element continuum example—showing that this geometry is a strong, task‑dependent inductive bias.

### Strain-Induced Helical Superconductivity and the Zero-Field Diode Effect

**Authors:** Raigo Nagashima, J\"org Schmalian

- Strain mixes dominant \(s\)-wave with subdominant two‑component pairing, enabling Lifshitz coupling that drives a transition to a helical superconducting state.
- The helical state breaks time‑reversal symmetry, producing a zero‑field superconducting diode effect that is non‑monotonic in strain and anisotropic to current direction.
- Reversing principal strain rotates the diode direction by \(90^\circ\); shear strain causes continuous rotation, offering strain‑controlled tuning of the diode.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13380)

### Superconducting Diode Effect in Josephson $\varphi_0$ Junction

**Authors:** A. Janalizadeh, Y. M. Shukrinov, M. R. Kolahchi

- Investigates superconducting diode effect in a φ₀ Josephson junction with a ferromagnetic weak link and Rashba spin‑orbit coupling.
- Extends the free energy with higher‑order gradient terms to handle strong exchange fields (h ≫ T_c) where the conventional Ginzburg‑Landau expansion fails.
- Shows that linear gradient coupling creates finite‑momentum Cooper pairing, and its interplay with higher‑order terms produces an asymmetric critical current (diode behavior).

### Tripartite entanglement in chaotic eigenstates

**Authors:** Junjia Zhang, Ramanjit Sohal, Shinsei Ryu

- Exact diagonalization of the mixed‑field Ising model validates the analytical predictions.
