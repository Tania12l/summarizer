**Authors:** Jeff Leiberton, Marcos Rigol

### Rejuvenation versus overaging: The effect of cyclic loading/unloading on the segmental dynamics of PMMA glasses

**Authors:** Trevor Bennin, Enran Xing, Josh Ricci, M. D. Ediger

- Cyclic tensile loading (5000 cycles, 003–007 strain, Tg – 10 K to Tg – 25 K) did not induce overaging in PMMA; segmental dynamics were unchanged or accelerated.
- Relaxation times after cycling returned to a common time scale comparable to the aging time, indicating retention of the material’s original age rather than full memory erasure.
- Probe reorientation measurements revealed deformation‐induced changes more sensitively than mechanical tests, yet no overaging was detected in either optical or mechanical data.
