### Hydrodynamization in 1D Bose gases at nonzero temperature

**Authors:** Jeff Leiberton, Marcos Rigol

- Hydrodynamization of a 1D hard‑core Bose gas after a Bragg‑pulse quench is examined at finite temperature.
- The hydrodynamic coherence time is temperature‑independent, while higher temperatures reduce the damping and prethermalization times.
- The shortening is linked to a broadened rapidity distribution, leading to a defined nonzero‑temperature dephasing time.

### Rejuvenation versus overaging: The effect of cyclic loading/unloading on the segmental dynamics of PMMA glasses
