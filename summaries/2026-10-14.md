### Quantum-magneto oscillations in a supramolecular Mn(II)-[3 x 3] grid

**Authors:** O. Waldmann, S. Carretta, P. Santini, R. Koch, A. G. M. Jansen, G. Amoretti, R. Caciuffo, L. Zhao, L. K. Thompson

- High‑field torque magnetometry on Mn(II)-[3 × 3] grid reveals oscillatory torque vs. field above 5 T.
- Oscillations observed at 3 He temperatures, indicating quantum‑magneto effects.
- A theoretical model successfully reproduces the magneto‑oscillation behavior.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401304v1)

### Ferromagnetism in Fe-doped SnO2 thin films

**Authors:** J. M. D. Coey, A. P. Douvalis, C. B. Fitzgerald, M. Venkatesan

- Fe‑doped SnO₂ thin films are transparent ferromagnets with TC ≈ 610 K and Ms ≈ 2 Am²/kg.
- Mossbauer spectra reveal high‑spin Fe³⁺; only ~23 % of Fe ions are magnetically ordered, each carrying ~8 µB.
- Ferromagnetism attributed to Fe³⁺ coupling through an electron trapped in an oxygen vacancy (F‑center).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401293v1)

### Ultrafast Conductivity Dynamics in Pentacene Probed using Terahertz Spectroscopy

**Authors:** V. K. Thorsmolle, R. D. Averitt, X. Chi, D. J. Hilton, D. L. Smith, A. P. Ramirez, A. J. Taylor

- Measured picosecond photoconductivity in pentacene crystals via optical‑pump THz‑probe.
- Mobility drops from ~4 cm²/Vs at 30 K to ~2 cm²/Vs at room temperature.
- THz signal indicates rapid (< ps) trapping of photoexcited carriers, likely by excitons, polarons, or impurities.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401296v1)

### Phases of a rotating Bose-Einstein condensate with anharmonic confinement

**Authors:** A. D. Jackson, G. M. Kavoulakis, E. Lundh

- Identified three phases (multiple, single, mixed quantization) in a rotating BEC with quadratic‑plus‑quartic confinement.
- Derived a universal phase diagram; continuous transitions exact for weak coupling and small anharmonicity.
- Variational predictions agree with Gross‑Pitaevskii numerical solutions.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401292v1)

### Observation of narrow fluorescence from doubly driven four-level atoms at room temperature

**Authors:** Uday Kumar Khan, Jimmy Sebastian, N. Kamaraju, Andal Narayanan, R. Srinivasan, Hema Ramachandran

- Narrow fluorescence peaks appear in Rb‑85 under dual‑laser 3D molasses; one laser fixed detuned from cooling transition, the other scanned over repumping lines.
- Peaks form symmetric pairs whose separation increases with cooling‑laser detuning; large detunings yield additional small peaks.
- A simple model explains the observed spectral features.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401280v1)

### Origin of asymmetric reversal modes in ferromagnetic/antiferromagnetic multilayers

**Authors:** B. Beckmann, U. Nowak, K. D. Usadel

- Asymmetric reversal modes appear in certain exchange‑bias multilayers.
- Domain‑state simulations show the effect depends on the angle between the AFM easy axis and the applied field.
- At specific angles the FM reverses symmetrically via coherent rotation; at others it reverses asymmetrically, sometimes producing zero perpendicular magnetization.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401232v1)

### Vortex waves and the onset of turbulence in $^3$He-B

**Authors:** K. L. Henderson, C. F. Barenghi

- Finne et al. identified the intrinsic turbulence onset criterion \(q=\alpha/(1-\alpha')<1\) in \(^{3}\)He‑B.
- The criterion corresponds to marginally damped Kelvin waves; for \(q>1\) waves cannot grow to trigger reconnections.
- Analysis of rotating‑superfluid modes confirms \(q=1\) as the crossover between damped and propagating Kelvin waves in the long‑wavelength limit.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401212v1)

### A hierarchy of exact lower bounds on the Laughlin spectral gap

**Authors:** Michael G. Scheer, Eslam Khalaf

- Uses Knabe’s inequality and a finite SDP to compute exact lower bounds on the Laughlin spectral gap for any flux number, including the thermodynamic limit.
- The SDP size depends only on cutoff parameters; an algorithm produces exact feasible points, turning numerical bounds into rigorous results.
- Demonstrates rank stability of the SDP as flux → ∞, enabling extension of the method to other pseudopotentials such as Moore‑Read and Read‑Rezayi.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18854v1)

### Emergent Loop Geometry in Gauge-Equivariant Graph Neural Networks

**Authors:** Ali Rayat, Gia-Wei Chern

- G‑equivariant GNNs generate ordered gauge‑link products via local message passing, forming closed lattice walks.
- Depth drives rapid contour‑length growth but only modest spatial expansion, yielding long paths confined to compact regions.
- Loop‑expanded fermionic observables show high‑order dependence on nearby gauge fields preserves locality, illustrating depth’s dual role in reach and representation richness.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18742v1)

### Superexchange coupling between a dilute Gd3+ probe and orphan spins in a valence-bond glass

**Authors:** Patrick Colgan, Artur Solodovnyk, Jeremy P. Carlo, Scott Dietrich

- Gd³⁺ EPR linewidth in Ba₂YMoO₆ is twice that of Ba₂YNbO₆, implying ~2 K superexchange with orphan spins.
- Dipolar coupling is too weak; transferred f‑d superexchange dominates the static broadening.
- The half‑field Δm_S = ±2 satellite collapses in the magnetic host, indicating dynamical dephasing while the g‑factor stays unchanged, evidencing random internal fields.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18722v1)

### Explicitly correlated ground states with a feedback-free variational hybrid algorithm

**Authors:** Benjamin Corbett, Akimasa Miyake

- Hybrid scheme: quantum prepares a low‑entanglement momentum‑space MPS, classical rotates to real space, and the Jastrow correlator is optimized entirely in post‑processing without quantum‑classical feedback.
- Tested on a 32‑qubit 4×4 Hubbard lattice (bond dimension 4 MPS): achieved 16 % relative energy error, reduced to 5 % after calibration.
- Outperforms other feedback‑free hybrids, needing fewer samples than quantum‑selected CI and fewer two‑qubit gates than quantum Krylov.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18681v1)

### Altermagnetic Ground States on the Ammann--Beenker Tiling

**Authors:** SeungBeom Hong, YunHo Kim, Moon Jip Park, GiBaik Sim

- Exact altermagnetic ground states identified for a classical Heisenberg model on the Ammann–Beenker tiling using the parity of its 4‑D hypercubic parent lattice.
- The ground state is coplanar altermagnet with spin‑group symmetry \([C_{2x}\parallel C_{8z}]\); translational symmetry is absent, so conventional momentum‑space spin splitting is inaccessible.
- Coupling to itinerant electrons produces spin‑resolved transport with \(g\)-wave symmetry, confirming altermagnetic behavior in this nonperiodic system.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18441v1)

### Coherent Phonon Generation from Prethermal Carrier Populations in a Semiconductor

**Authors:** Mingyu Shi, Matteo Savoini, Patrick Friebel, Andrea Annunziata, Thomas Metzger, Yunpei Deng, Laura Cattaneo, Steven L. Johnson

- Coherent A₁ phonon amplitude in Te varies only weakly with photon energy across the band gap, challenging thermalized‑carrier models.
- The spectral response is reproduced by a prethermal force weighted by optical matrix elements and deformation potentials, showing transition selectivity.
- Fits give a transition‑state lifetime of a few femtoseconds, underscoring prethermal carrier populations as the main driver of coherent‑phonon generation.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18279v1)
