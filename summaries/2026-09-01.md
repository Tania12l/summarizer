### Phase stability, charge ordering, and charge-liquid formation in the Falicov-Kimball model on triangular and kagome lattices

**Authors:** Ammar Nejati, Marvin Lenk

- Spinless Falicov‑Kimball model on triangular and kagome lattices mapped with Markov‑chain Monte Carlo.
- Triangular lattice hosts Anderson, Mott, and CDW phases; a charge‑liquid regime appears between weak coupling and CDW, driven by interaction‑mediated nesting.
- Kagome lattice shows no CDW, only an insulating (likely Mott) ground state with hints of a zero‑temperature quantum transition, highlighting lattice geometry and particle‑hole symmetry effects.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28898)

### Enhanced Diffusion from Twist-Angle Disorder

**Authors:** Nicole S Ticea, Yi-Ming Wu

- Twist‑angle disorder in twisted bilayers acts like a curl of interlayer displacement, creating spatially correlated Hamiltonian variations.
- This geometric scattering suppresses momentum relaxation compared to white‑noise disorder, enhancing diffusion.
- The system maps onto a non‑linear sigma model, enabling quantitative analysis of transport and ordering effects.

### Duality Between Twist-Angle Disorder and Non-Hermitian Disorder

**Authors:** Yi-Ming Wu, Nicole S. Ticea

- Twist‑angle disorder in moiré heterobilayers is modeled as a finite‑range uniform twist that randomly deforms beyond that range, with a probability distribution penalizing large deviations.
- Replica‑trick averaging yields alternating‑sign correlations between replicas, strongest near domain boundaries and along specific directions.
- A duality maps these correlations to a non‑Hermitian disorder model for fermions, showing that twist‑angle disorder can evade Anderson localization differently from conventional disorder.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29064)
