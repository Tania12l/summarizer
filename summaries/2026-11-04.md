### Why strong spin-orbit coupling does not destabilize the Dirac spin liquid in the Yb delafossites

**Authors:** Yasir Iqbal

- Strong spin‑orbit coupling in Yb delafossites generates only irrelevant four‑fermion operators, so the U(Dirac spin liquid remains stable.
- Inversion symmetry eliminates the Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya channel, forbidding mass, density, or current terms at all orders.
- Interlayer monopole processes appear only in rhombohedral stacking and become relevant only below tens of mK, while at the 120° transition the anisotropy drives a phase change.

### Spin-orbit-locked symmetry and the stability of the triangular Dirac spin liquid in the ytterbium delafossites

**Authors:** Yasir Iqbal

- Spin‑orbit coupling forbids all Dirac mass, density, and current operators, preserving the U(Dirac spin liquid on the triangular lattice.
- Linear and quadratic spin‑orbit terms generate only irrelevant higher‑spin quartics, so the Heisenberg spin liquid remains Lorentz‑scalar stable.
- Rhombohedral stacking introduces triplet‑monopole tunneling; at the 120° transition the easy‑plane anisotropy becomes relevant, selecting a chiral‑Ising ordered phase.
