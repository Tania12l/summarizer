### Mpemba effect as an entropy-production race:Finite-amplitude bounds on crossing times in Markovian relaxation

**Authors:** Mesfin Taye

- Mpemba effect arises when two Markovian preparations excite different relaxation modes, modeled as an entropy‑production race.
- The first crossing of relative entropies occurs when the accumulated difference of normalized entropy‑production rates equals the initial logarithmic deficit, yielding lower bounds via entropy‑decay inequalities.
- Finite‑amplitude crossing bounds are derived without small‑deviation expansions; for a reversible three‑state system the crossing time is tightly bounded \(1659\le t_M\le1674\).
