### Hidden convexity of the Eliashberg condensation energy: existence, uniqueness and continuity of the superconducting state

**Authors:** Davis Rens

- Hidden convexity of the condensation energy in the first quadrant yields existence and uniqueness (up to gauge) of the superconducting minimizer for any positive, non‑increasing power‑law interaction.
- The normal state is the unique minimizer above the critical temperature; a positive solution of the nonlinear Eliashberg equations appears precisely when the normal state becomes linearly unstable.
- For temperature‑dependent families with pointwise continuity and uniform decay, the minimizing density varies continuously in weighted ℓ¹, confirming the thermodynamic conjectures for the γ‑model, Einstein phonons, and general spectra.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21773)
