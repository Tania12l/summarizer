### LatticeMind: A Validation-Gated Agentic Framework for Density Functional Theory Workflows

**Authors:** Nathaniel M. Vegh, Yu Zhu, Rapha\"el J. Prentki, F\'elix Beaudoin, Shuaishuai Yuan, Hong Guo

- LatticeMind converts natural‑language requests into executable DFT workflows, using deterministic pre‑execution validators and bounded repair to ensure correct file handoffs and dependencies.
- On 44 RESCU and 19 VASP benchmarks, it reproduces every reference value, with 119/132 runs passing on the first validated plan and the rest after a single repair cycle.
- Ablations reveal that the workflow contract and pre‑execution validation are the primary reliability mechanisms, outperforming a conventional trial‑and‑repair baseline.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26869)

### Vortex matter as a magnetically programmable metasurface for tuning near-field radiative heat transfer

**Authors:** Siddharth Sangi, Pramod Reddy, Shanhui Fan

- Establishes vortex matter as a magnetically programmable thermal metasurface, enabling large‑range nanoscale heat flow control.

### Zeeman Quantum Geometry of the Layer Pseudo-spin

**Authors:** Bingxuan Jiang, Shihao Zhang

- Defines a mixed tensor of interband Berry connection and layer matrix element that describes the quantum geometry of layer pseudo‑spin and its response to in‑plane electric fields.
- Shows that in a minimal two‑band model the quantum metric and Zeeman–Berry curvature are nonzero yet their Brillouin‑zone integrals vanish, so a linear drive produces only a moiré‑periodic intracell polarization pattern.
- Quantifies local dipole modulation in twisted MoTe₂ bilayers and identifies rectified, helicity‑odd, and third‑harmonic channels that generate a net higher‑order response.

### Heat-current-induced nonreciprocal phonon damping

**Authors:** Tingwei Deng, Xianyong Ding, Rui Wang, Xiangang Wan

- Heat current induces nonreciprocal anharmonic phonon damping in centrosymmetric nonmagnetic crystals.
- Linewidth correction is linear in the temperature gradient and odd under wave‑vector reversal.
- First‑principles MgO and diamond calculations show mode‑dependent corrections; reversing the heat current swaps broadening and narrowing.

### Finite-temperature spectral functions of lattice polarons with process tensors

**Authors:** Qi Gao, Yuan Wan

- Developed a process‑tensor method that expresses the finite‑temperature phonon influence functional as an elementwise exponential of a low‑rank matrix‑product state, enabling efficient electron worldline summation.
- Introduced an embedding that unifies worldlines of different propagation times into a single matrix‑product state, producing the full propagator in one calculation; benchmarked against 1D Holstein polaron DMRG and extended to converged 2D spectra.
- Showed that increasing temperature transfers spectral weight from coherent peaks to an incoherent background in Holstein polaron spectral functions.

### Symmetry Enriched Criticality from Kennedy Tasaki Self-Duality

**Authors:** Mingyuan Chen, Sheng Yang, Xue-Jia Yu

- Symmetry‑enriched critical points between SPT and SSB phases are governed by Kennedy–Tasaki self‑duality, a noninvertible self‑dual symmetry.
- The KT transformation swaps SPT and SSB phases, giving the critical points nontrivial topological features.
- Numerical results on $\mathbb{Z}_n\times\mathbb{Z}_n$ cluster qudit chains confirm the mechanism, and a duality criterion extends to higher dimensions.
