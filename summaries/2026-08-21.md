### Embedding Paired Free-Fermion Gaussian States into Gutzwiller-Projected Bardeen--Cooper--Schrieffer Wave Functions

**Authors:** Ryui Kaneko

- Embeds any even‑parity spinless fermionic Gaussian state into a Gutzwiller‑projected BCS state of spinful Abrikosov fermions.
- Demonstrated exactly for the 1D transverse‑field Ising model.
- Supplies controlled initial states for variational Monte‑Carlo studies of non‑integrable systems.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18168)

### Chern insulator boundary criticality

**Authors:** Benjamin Moy, Eduardo Fradkin

- Critical Hall conductivity σ_xy = ½ e²/h at the C=1 transition, described by a massless Dirac fermion with a parity anomaly.
- Chiral edge mode delocalizes into the bulk at criticality, yet boundary fermion correlators retain chiral structure and bulk scaling dimension, matching the anomaly.
- Generalizes to higher Chern numbers and 3+1 d TI transitions, identifying parity‑odd terms in current and stress‑tensor correlators via residual conformal symmetry.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18199)

### Learning constructive models of emergent systems

**Authors:** Bipul Pandey, Caden Proctor, Vinay Ramanathan, Nico Roth, Arjun S. Raman

- Introduces Layer‑Restricted Boltzmann Machine (LRBM) that constrains information flow across entropic scales to construct emergent systems.
- Demonstrates hierarchical encoding: low layers capture global structure, higher layers encode fine‑grained features across images, language, and enzyme sequences.
- Validated by designing 1,130 chorismate mutase variants; functional enzymes achieved up to 53% sequence divergence, showing fold emerges at intermediate layers but function requires all layers.

### Finite-temperature Green's function cluster expansion from thermofield doubles: Breakdown of the polaron picture

**Authors:** M. R. Carbone, S. Fomichev, B. Kloss, A. J. Millis, M. Berciu, D. R. Reichman, J. Sous

- Finite‑temperature Green’s‑function cluster expansion (FTGFCE) combines a generalized cluster expansion with the thermofield‑double formalism to compute single‑particle Green’s functions of polarons at finite temperature.
- The method delivers momentum‑resolved spectral functions, dispersions, lifetimes, and effective masses for the Holstein polaron up to \(T/\Omega \sim 1\), agreeing quantitatively with finite‑temperature DMRG.
- FTGFCE is numerically exact in principle, avoids time evolution, and is implemented in an open‑source software package.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18267)

### Rare-earth spin textures and a route to electronic inhomogeneity in $Pr_2Ir_2O_7$

**Authors:** Kyle G Sherman, Michael J Lawler

- Monte‑Carlo spin‑ice Pr configurations coupled via Kondo exchange to an Ir Hartree‑Fock model reproduce surface Kondo‑inhomogeneity in Pr₂Ir₂O₇.
- The all‑in‑all‑out charge gap closes smoothly with J_K, and jellyfish monopole textures lower the insulating gap by ΔJ_Kc ≈ 03 t.
- Synthetic tunneling maps reveal island‑like Fermi‑level DOS variations that, through Doniach exponential sensitivity, tip the balance between Kondo screening and magnetic order, explaining the observed coexistence.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18301)

### Engineering kekule superconductivity from layer-selective interactions in rhombohedral graphene

**Authors:** Hung Dinh Nguyen, Yafis Barlas

- Finite‑momentum Kekulé superconductivity arises in rhombohedral multilayer graphene from layer‑dependent opposite‑sign interactions, preserving time‑reversal and inversion symmetry.
- The state appears only above a critical carrier density when a Kane‑Mele mass is present, with opposite‑chirality condensates in the two valleys.
- Phase diagram, Tc versus chirality index \(J\) and mass \(\lambda\), and the BKT transition temperature are mapped, demonstrating orbital‑dependent interactions favor finite‑momentum pairing.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18337)

### Topology is not silent in the transport noise of multiband bosons

**Authors:** Zhi-Wei Wang, Samuel L. Braunstein

- Topological Berry curvature cancels in electron Hall‑current noise at a Fermi surface, but not for thermally populated bosonic bands.
- Boson noise contains an occupation‑weighted dispersion of Chern numbers, a new term absent at zero temperature.
- Derived from a fluctuating Boltzmann equation; measurable only via an equilibrium sum‑rule protocol, not in driven experiments.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18527)

### Universal thermal breakdown of polaron coherence in one, two, and three dimensions

**Authors:** Jeet Shannigrahi, Janez Bon\v{c}a, Mona Berciu

- Polaron coherence universally breaks down at \(T \approx 5\,\Omega\), independent of dimensionality or coupling strength.
- Effective mass \(m^*\) and inverse lifetime \(1/\tau\) rise monotonically with temperature until the quasiparticle peak dissolves into an incoherent continuum.
- A finite‑temperature Momentum‑Average approximation produces a self‑energy that matches exact 1D benchmarks (VED‑FTLM, DMRG) across all coupling regimes.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18529)

### Transitions and Critical Divergences in Periodically Hopping Modulated Su-Schrieffer-Heeger Chains

**Authors:** Surajit Mandal, Satyaki Kar

- CRG analysis of SSH chains shows curvature‑function divergence at Δ=0 (two‑site) and at |Δ/t|=√2 (four‑site) marking topological transitions.
- Δ=0 exhibits faster divergence and larger correlation‑length exponent than the Dirac‑like |Δ/t|=√2 point, placing them in distinct universality classes.
- Edge modes decay slowly into the bulk near Δ=0 but quickly near |Δ/t|=√2; the study extends similarly to eight‑site periodic modulations.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18706)

### Sign-problem-resilient singular-value probe in determinant quantum Monte Carlo

**Authors:** Wen Chen, Rubem Mondaini

- Uses adjacent gap ratio of low‑lying singular values of the fermion matrix to detect interaction‑driven transitions in determinant QMC.
- Accurately tracks the Dirac‑semimetal to antiferromagnetic Mott transition in the sign‑free honeycomb Hubbard model.
- Remains robust in the sign‑affected Haldane‑Hubbard model, surviving phase reweighting and locating the transition near earlier estimates.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19028)

### Chiral bosonic mean-field Ansatz and spin dynamics in spin-1 Kitaev magnets

**Authors:** Daiki Sasamoto, Arnaud Ralko, Jaime Merino, Joji Nasu

- Introduces a triplet‑pairing φ_t = π/2 mean‑field Ansatz for the S = 1 Kitaev model, extended to weak symmetric Γ, Γ′ exchanges.
- Calculates the dynamical spin structure factor; the π/2‑flux Ansatz yields a flatter spectrum and stronger suppression of long‑range correlations than the 0‑flux Ansatz, matching short‑range Kitaev physics.
- Demonstrates that spectral flatness is tied to short‑range spin correlations and explores the effect of weak Γ, Γ′ perturbations near the pure Kitaev limit.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19178)
