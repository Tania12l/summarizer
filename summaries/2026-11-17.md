### Quantum-magneto oscillations in a supramolecular Mn(II)-[3 x 3] grid

**Authors:** O. Waldmann, S. Carretta, P. Santini, R. Koch, A. G. M. Jansen, G. Amoretti, R. Caciuffo, L. Zhao, L. K. Thompson

- Oscillations observed at 3 He temperatures, indicating quantum‑magneto effects.
- A theoretical model successfully reproduces the magneto‑oscillation behavior.

### Ferromagnetism in Fe-doped SnO2 thin films

**Authors:** J. M. D. Coey, A. P. Douvalis, C. B. Fitzgerald, M. Venkatesan

- Fe‑doped SnO₂ thin films are transparent ferromagnets with TC ≈ 610 K and Ms ≈ 2 Am²/kg.
- Mossbauer spectra reveal high‑spin Fe³⁺; only ~23 % of Fe ions are magnetically ordered, each carrying ~8 µB.
- Ferromagnetism attributed to Fe³⁺ coupling through an electron trapped in an oxygen vacancy (F‑center).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401293v1)

### Observation of narrow fluorescence from doubly driven four-level atoms at room temperature

**Authors:** Uday Kumar Khan, Jimmy Sebastian, N. Kamaraju, Andal Narayanan, R. Srinivasan, Hema Ramachandran

- Narrow fluorescence peaks appear in Rb‑85 under dual‑laser 3D molasses; one laser fixed detuned from cooling transition, the other scanned over repumping lines.

### Vortex waves and the onset of turbulence in $^3$He-B

**Authors:** K. L. Henderson, C. F. Barenghi

- Finne et al. identified the intrinsic turbulence onset criterion \(q=\alpha/(1-\alpha')<1\) in \(^{3}\)He‑B.
- The criterion corresponds to marginally damped Kelvin waves; for \(q>1\) waves cannot grow to trigger reconnections.
- Analysis of rotating‑superfluid modes confirms \(q=1\) as the crossover between damped and propagating Kelvin waves in the long‑wavelength limit.

### Hyperkagome antiferromagnetism in the iron garnet Almandine Fe$_3$Al$_2$Si$_3$O$_{12}$

**Authors:** Minnong Zhang, Harald O. Jeschke, Daniel Guterding

- Fe₃Al₂Si₃O₁₂ is a spin‑2 hyperkagome antiferromagnet with dominant nearest‑neighbor AF exchange and notable longer‑range interactions.
- Classical Monte Carlo (with quantum‑to‑classical rescaling) predicts a Néel transition at ≈8 K, yielding a non‑collinear toroidal octupolar ordered state.
- Calculated neutron diffraction amplitudes and dynamic structure factors provide quantitative predictions, linking almandine’s behavior to that of Gd₃Ga₅O₁₂.

**Authors:** Bernhard Jobst, Simone Warzel, Alexander Altland, Frank Pollmann

- Rigorous bounds show localization at strong disorder; numerics on exponentially large trees reveal a finite‑disorder transition.

### Hund physics in the quarter-filled two-band Hubbard-Kanamori model: Crystal-field splitting and non-local Correlations

**Authors:** Diego Florez-Ablan, Carlos Mejuto-Zaera, Massimo Capone

- Weak Hund’s metal survives at quarter filling, robust to crystal‑field splittings up to ~¼ bandwidth.
- Single‑site DMFT reproduces the phase boundary; cluster DMFT confirms the picture but shifts the Mott transition to lower U.
- In the insulating phase, nearest‑neighbor ferromagnetic and antiferro‑orbital correlations emerge, seen in mutual information; metallic intersite correlations remain weak and J‑insensitive.

### Genuine multipartite entanglement in ring-shaped Hubbard models

**Authors:** K. Shahzad, S. Pittalis, G. Goldoni, R. Di Felice, F. Troiani

- On‑site repulsion suppresses charge fluctuations, enhancing genuine multipartite entanglement (GME) of extracted spin states.
- At half filling, GME stays robust with only weak decay of detection probability as spin number increases; temperatures below the singlet–triplet gap preserve GME, and doping can even boost it.
- In the extended Hubbard model, the spin‑to‑charge‑density‑wave transition abruptly suppresses both GME and detection probability.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20756v1)
