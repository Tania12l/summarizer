### Uniform meandric systems as percolation on planar maps: conjectures and a numerical study

**Authors:** David Wei

- Numerical analysis of uniform meandric systems via random walk encoding, viewed as critical percolation on random planar maps.
- Strong negative association found between opposite open crossings; conjectures on asymptotic box‑crossing behavior.
- Introduces a law‑preserving peak/valley flip dynamics, showing percolation decorrelates faster than the underlying map.

### Magnon chiral damping beyond the Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interaction

**Authors:** Darpa Narayan Basu, Subhadip Ghosh, Peter M. Oppeneer, Alexander Mook, Ritwik Mondal

- Antisymmetric Gilbert damping alone generates magnon chiral damping, independent of Dzyaloshinskii‑Moriya interactions.
- Effective damping becomes wave‑vector dependent: scalar part ∝ k², antisymmetric part ∝ k, reproducing experimental nonreciprocity.
- In two‑sublattice magnets, cross‑sublattice antisymmetric damping yields nonreciprocal magnon relaxation without DM.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03766)

### From Dirac surface response to Casimir forces in Bumblebee-CFJ electrodynamics

**Authors:** F. C. E. Lima

- Casimir force between planar massive Dirac sheets and a Lorentz‑violating Bumblebee–CFJ vector sector is computed with Lifshitz scattering using the one‑loop polarization tensor.
- Repulsion originates from CFJ‑induced TE/TM mode mixing and spatial dispersion, not from Hall conductivity; the surface response stays parity‑even.
- Increasing Dirac mass weakens the force and changes its long‑range scaling, while the CFJ parameter tunes attractive–repulsive transitions.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03768)

### Harnessing Measurement Error as a Thermodynamic Resource in Two-Reservoir Information Engines

**Authors:** Yang Xiao, Qian Zeng, Jin Wang

### Predicting and extending the bubble-free operating limit of capillary-fed water electrolysis

**Authors:** Farkhondeh Khodabandeh, Zhengmao Lu

- Bubble-free limit in capillary-fed electrolyzers is set by separator pore size, balancing nucleation supersaturation and electrolyte permeability.
- Reducing pore size from 8 µm to 2 µm raises bubble onset from 18 to 73 A cm⁻² and cuts cell voltage by 11 V at 1 A cm⁻².
- Below ~45 µm pores, electrolyte replenishment limits performance, causing drying before bubble formation.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03782)

### Information Entropy and Dissipation Potential in Stochastic Nambu Nonequilibrium Thermodynamics

**Authors:** So Katagiri, Tatsuaki Wada

- Links the Nambu dissipation potential \(S_{\mathrm{NB}}\) to information entropy in stochastic Nambu nonequilibrium thermodynamics.
- Shows that, with an Einstein‑type relation, the stationary Fokker–Planck distribution is exponential in \(S_{\mathrm{NB}}\), making it the quasipotential of the steady state.
- Distinguishes \(S_{\mathrm{NB}}\) from Shannon entropy, introduces a Massieu‑type interpretation, a Nambu canonical distribution, and derives fluctuation covariances via a saddle‑point approximation.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03790)

### Surface scalar plasmons on curved interfaces: a venue for analogue gravity

**Authors:** Matheus E. Pereira

- Derived a massive Klein–Gordon equation for scalar surface plasmons on curved interfaces, revealing a geometric potential.
- Showed that specific curvature can cancel the effective mass, allowing massless SSP propagation.
- Applied the formalism to an analogue black hole, computing quasinormal mode frequencies and highlighting curvature‑controlled plasmon transport.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03813)

### State-selective entanglement and point-gap topology in the unidirectional Bose-Hubbard chain

**Authors:** Chuan-Fu Lin, Yu-Chin Tzeng, Po-Yao Chang

- Repulsive interactions suppress skin accumulation in the unidirectional Bose–Hubbard chain, driving a superfluid–Mott transition in the ground state.
- The ground state becomes uniformly filled with decreasing entanglement, whereas the steady state (largest‑imaginary‑energy eigenstate) shows a nonmonotonic entanglement crossover with four spatial maxima tied to competing doublon–holon sectors.
- Even at strong interaction the many‑body winding about the origin stays nonzero; projected doublon–holon manifolds reproduce the entropy profile, but the winding becomes ill‑defined as \(U/t\to\infty\).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04331)

### Does a pole in the self-energy imply a violation of the Luttinger theorem?

**Authors:** Artem Abanov, Andrey Chubukov

- Luttinger theorem can remain valid even when the self‑energy has a pole.
- A model with two gapped bands plus a narrow residual Fermi‑liquid band (small residue Z) yields a Green’s function with two zeros and three poles.
- In Model I (bandwidth renormalized by Z) the Luttinger–Ward analysis holds for any Z > 0; in Model II it requires Z to exceed a density‑dependent critical value.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04663)

### Complete Entanglement Structure of the Kitaev Honeycomb Spin Liquid from Exact Tensor Networks

**Authors:** Hidehiro Saito, Chisa Hotta

- Exact 2‑D tensor‑network construction of all Kitaev honeycomb eigenstates via sequential quantum‑number and Gutzwiller projections (local MPS, \(D=2\)).
- Ground and excited states share bond dimension but differ in Schmidt spectra: area‑law vs volume‑law entanglement, with topological entropy appearing only after Gutzwiller projection.
- Projection collapses wavefunctions into a single gauge sector, demonstrating that excited states are also topologically ordered and extending the approach to other \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) spin liquids.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05395)
