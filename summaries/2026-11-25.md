### Tunneling signatures of the Lifshitz transition in a superconducting vortex

**Authors:** Alex Levchenko

- Zeeman field \(B^*\sim\Delta^2/E_F\) drives a Lifshitz transition: the lowest vortex miniband crosses the Fermi level, creating a spin‑polarized 1D Fermi sea.
- Tunneling spectra show zero‑bias conductance absent below \(B^*\), peaking at \(B^*\) with a \(1/\sqrt{B-B^*}\) van Hove singularity, then falling; a second miniband onset appears farther from the core.
- Projected interactions give a Luttinger parameter ≈ 1, implying a weakly interacting 1D electron liquid inside the vortex core

### Non-integer high harmonic generation from many-body nonadiabatic gating

**Authors:** Mingyao Guo, Luting Wang, Murali Suganya, Biqiong Yu, Martin Greven, Sritharan Krishnamoorthi, Raman Sankar, Yao Wang, David Hsieh

- Giant blueshifted, non‑integer high harmonics observed in Sr₂CuO₃ under mid‑IR excitation, violating the usual integer‑multiple rule.

### Localization in one-dimensional magnetic quasicrystals

**Authors:** Andrew Osborne, Rhine Samajdar, Andrew A. Houck

- Exact diagonalization shows on‑site interactions delocalize localized states, highlighting the joint role of magnetic interference and interactions in transport.

### Origin of quadrupolar order in the 5d$^1$ double perovskite Ba$_2$MgReO$_6$

**Authors:** Dario Fiore Mosca, Leonid V. Pourovskii

- First‑principles calculation of superexchange, quadrupole–quadrupole, vibronic, and virtual‑excitation terms in Ba₂MgReO₆.
- Microscopic Hamiltonian reproduces the observed ferroic z² quadrupolar order and lattice distortions quantitatively.
- Ferroic z² order originates from a linear‑quartic coupling via virtual J_eff = ½ excitations, driven by vibronic interactions.

### Ground state and thermal phase diagrams of the magnetized spin-$1/2$ XXZ antiferromagnet on the triangular lattice

**Authors:** Owan Cho, Seungho Lee, Hyun-Yong Lee, SungBin Lee

- Ground‑state phase diagram mapped from Ising to Heisenberg limits, showing a stable Ψ supersolid below saturation for all anisotropies.
- Finite‑temperature study finds a bond‑nematic phase above the zero‑temperature plateau–Ψ boundary in the easy‑axis regime, with inequivalent bonds but no transverse spin order.
- Hard‑core boson mapping explains the nematic as thermally delocalized kink excitations aligning domain walls of up‑up‑down order via a Z₃ registry.
