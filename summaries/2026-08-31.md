### Exact branch-transfer criterion for common-mode Thomson heat cancellation in thermoelectric couples

**Authors:** Peng Kang, Da Wan, Shulin Bai, Wei Yin, Peng Wang, Chenglong Wen, Zhen Li, Yu Liu, Lei Zheng, Li-Dong Zhao

- Exact branch‑transfer criterion: common‑mode Thomson heat cancels iff the differential thermopower measures of the p‑ and n‑branches are equal.
- In the linear‑common‑mode limit, matching \(R_i/K_i^{\text{leg}}\) suffices for cancellation, even with non‑identical legs.
- Derived array law for split thermal pads: \(\Delta Q_{c,\Sigma}=\sum_j C_j I_j \Delta T_{c,j}\); for series elements with isothermal hot pairs, \(I\Delta V_\Sigma=-\Delta Q_{c,\Sigma}\).

### Asphericity Lifts the Degeneracy Among Ordering Mechanisms on Curved Surfaces

**Authors:** An\v{z}e Bo\v{z}i\v{c}

- Spherical surfaces hide the signature of different ordering mechanisms; asphericity lifts this degeneracy.
- On an ellipsoid, mechanisms separate into distinct powers of the distance to the tangent plane, readable from the structure‑factor quadrupole and radius of gyration.
- Riesz s‑energy shows a sharp transition at a marginal power, while screened Coulomb potential lets ionic strength continuously tune the exponent, enabling experimental control of the conductor‑to‑packing crossover.

### Topological signatures in the quench dynamics of periodically driven quantum systems

**Authors:** Ashutosh Dubey, Hironmoy Pratihar, Diptiman Sen, Arijit Kundu

- Quench of graphene by circularly polarized light produces a period‑averaged edge bond current that oscillates around a dc value, signaling an induced topological phase.
- In an isolated ribbon the dc current equals Floquet band currents weighted by their occupations; coupling to a fermionic bath causes the current and conductance to rise then saturate to a nonequilibrium steady state.
- In the weak‑coupling limit the summed conductance is quantized, revealing the number of edge modes at the zero‑quasienergy and Floquet‑zone‑boundary gaps, confirmed by two‑terminal transport in a finite‑size model.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27588)

### Turning Zeeman splitting into switchable charge polarization in a double quantum dot

**Authors:** Chi Sun, Jacob Linder

### Spin-Selective Spectral Flattening and Wave-Packet Dynamics in a Flux-Engineered Lieb Lattice

**Authors:** Nana Chang, Xiaoji Zhou, Yanglin Zhou, Song Ci

- Spin‑dependent Peierls phases give ↑ and ↓ components effective fluxes α↑=½, α↓=0 in a Lieb lattice.
- At α0=αs=¼ the ↑ spectrum has two flat zero‑energy subbands, while ↓ remains dispersive, so full Aharonov–Bohm caging fails.
- Time evolution shows ↓ propagates freely, ↑ is strongly localized; reversing the flux swaps the slow/fast channels and the effect persists under moderate detuning.
