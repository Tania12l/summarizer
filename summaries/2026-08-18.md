### Trimer Thouless Pump: Topology, Symmetries, and Multigap Structure

**Authors:** Rittwik Chatterjee

- Introduces a minimal three‑band “Trimer Thouless Pump” exhibiting multigap topological transport.
- Shows quantized charge pumped via Berry‑curvature hotspots at Dirac‑like avoided crossings, with the middle band mediating flux exchange while having zero net Chern number.
- Maps phase diagram versus central‑site detuning, predicts band‑selective topological invariant transfer, and proposes ultracold‑atom realization in optical superlattices.

### Spectral preservation under momentum-dependent similarity transformations in non-Hermitian lattice systems

**Authors:** Ye Ma

- Momentum‑dependent similarity transforms preserve spectra in finite non‑Hermitian lattices only under specific conditions.
- For traceless \(2\times2\) Hamiltonians, necessary and sufficient criteria for skew‑diagonalization via constant similarity transforms are derived, with explicit matrices.
- Bulk spectral preservation requires the generalized Brillouin zone to lie inside the smallest zero of \(\det S(z)\) and no critical non‑Hermitian skin effect; two‑sidedness of \(S(z)\) only alters a finite number of boundary eigenvalues.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14775)

### No-Go Theorem and Routes towards Cavity-Enhanced Superconductivity

**Authors:** Qing-Dong Jiang

- Minimal cavity electrodynamics yields a no‑go theorem: vacuum fluctuations suppress superconductivity because paramagnetic exchange cannot outweigh diamagnetic renormalization.
- Enhancement is possible only by coupling an additional collective mode that boosts paramagnetic attraction or by weakening a competing order that limits superconductivity.
- Thus, practical cavity‑enhanced superconductivity requires an extra cavity‑coupled material degree of freedom to overcome the inherent suppression.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14784)

### Interaction driven charge transfer transitions in closely spaced graphene double layers

**Authors:** Kenneth A. Lin, Unmesh Ghorai, Kenji Watanabe, Takashi Taniguchi, Emanuel Tutuc, Rafi Bistritzer

- Charge transfer in graphene double layers proceeds through interaction‑driven phase transitions, not simple electrostatics.
- Intralayer exchange causes abrupt subband transfer, while interlayer exchange stabilizes coherent intermediate phases for gradual transfer.
- The process reconstructs the quasiparticle spectrum, making interlayer tunneling a probe of bias‑dependent interacting systems.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14805)

### Electronic structure and magnetic correlations in the epitaxially strained bilayer nickelate La$_3$Ni$_2$O$_{7}$

**Authors:** I. V. Leonov

- Orbital‑selective renormalization of Ni 3d states in La₃Ni₂O₇, with strong incoherence and proximity to localization.
- In‑plane strain (tensile or moderate compressive ≤ –2 %) boosts magnetic correlations; large compressive strain (~–4 %) induces a Lifshitz transition that removes the γ Fermi‑surface sheet and suppresses magnetism.
- Results support a spin‑ and charge‑density‑wave stripe instability driven by Fermi‑surface nesting, tunable by pressure or strain.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14810)

### Rapid supercurrent decay in Mn$_5$Si$_3$ Josephson junctions

**Authors:** Arjun Sapkota, Kurt Lorenzen, Tyler Kuhn, Juan Gomez, Demet Korucu, Robert M. Klaes, Reza Loloee, Norman O. Birge, Nathan Satchell

- Ic decays exponentially with ξ ≈ 31 nm, far shorter than in FeMn, Cr, NiMn junctions.
- No 0‑π transition detected; Ic(T) for a 1 nm barrier is smooth and monotonic.
- Rapid decay and barrier microstructure limit observable transitions; oriented crystalline barriers are required.

### Exact Thermoelectric Transport Coefficients and Figure of Merit for Graphene Photothermoelectric Devices from a Finite Zeta-Function Mott Series

**Authors:** Luis Daniel Villa Cortes, S. R. Valluri, Atul Jhalani, Ajay Soni, P. C. Deshmukh

- Finite zeta‑function Mott series yields exact thermoelectric coefficients for polynomial transport functions.
- Applied to graphene photothermoelectric devices (quadratic conductivity) gives closed‑form Seebeck, Lorenz ratio, and figure‑of‑merit, revealing a Seebeck peak and strong Wiedemann–Franz violation at higher temperatures.
- Disorder reduces performance and imposes an upper limit on the electronic figure of merit; radiative heat transport further affects the figure of merit.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14850)

### Physics-Informed Symbolic Regression for Predicting the Glass Transition Temperature of Alkali Borate Glasses

**Authors:** Leonardo dos Santos Vitoria, Marcio Luis Ferreira Nascimento, Susana de Souza Lalic, Daniel Roberto Cassar

- Physics‑informed symbolic regression yields a closed‑form Tg model for alkali borate glasses with 14–16 K RMSE.
- Using Rigid Unit Packing Fraction (RUPF) instead of Atomic Packing Fraction captures Tg–dissociation energy–packing interplay and avoids over‑rigidity at intermediate compositions.
- Fitted dissociation energies agree with the Makishima‑Mackenzie model; Monte‑Carlo uncertainty peaks near the boron anomaly region.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14853)

### Coherent Phonon Blocking in Superlattices

**Authors:** Anil Erol, Xiang Hua, Kirby Myers, Lawrence Friedman, Alexander Marakov, Melissa G. Loving, Joshua Shipman, Arsha Mamoozadeh, Sarah Millen, Ronald J. Warzoha, Jeremy Clark, Robert M. Young

**Authors:** M. G. Kim, C. Neupane, Y. Yu, R. Acevedo-Esteves, C. Nelson, D. Evans, E. D. Mun, D. F. Agterberg, J. -W. Kim

- XRMS reveals a commensurate antiferromagnetic structure (q = (0,0,) with Sm moments in the ab‑plane and ++–– stacking along c.
- Synchrotron XRD shows tetragonal symmetry down to 8 K; the c‑axis lattice parameter plateaus and rises near T_N, evidencing magnetoelastic coupling.
- Comparison with Pr, Nd, Gd analogues indicates that in‑plane moment orientation, magnetic frustration, and spin‑orbit/magnetoelastic differences govern the low‑temperature resistivity minimum.

**Authors:** Sen Niu, Rui-Zhen Huang

- Resulting states exhibit clean gapped correlations and finite transfer‑matrix correlation lengths for both a Chern insulator and a chiral spin liquid.
