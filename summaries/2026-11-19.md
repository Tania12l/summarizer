**Authors:** Bo Peng, Zeya Li, Han Wang, Shengdong You, Yangliu Wu, Wei Liu, Jianliang Xie, Haipeng Lu, Peiheng Zhou, Kian Ping Loh, Je-Geun Park, Hongtao Yuan, Longjiang Deng

### Trapped fermions frustrate multiterminal Josephson networks

**Authors:** Antonio Manesco, Karsten Flensberg

- Odd‑parity trijunctions with trapped fermions impose phase windings that frustrate multiterminal Josephson arrays.
- The frustrated network maps to a Kagome XY antiferromagnet, showing a BKT transition and 6e phase coherence that appears as a third‑harmonic in the current‑phase relation.
- Quantum phase fluctuations and spin‑orbit coupling suppress 6e coherence but induce spin correlations and entanglement between the trapped fermions.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23390)

**Authors:** Carlos M. Pedrosa, Jo\~ao P. C. Ferreira, Jo\~ao Pinho, Yu. V. Bludov, B. Amorim, Jo\~ao M. Viana Parente Lopes

### Real-Space Theory of Nonequilibrium Orbital Magnetization in Inhomogeneous Solids

**Authors:** Jo\~ao M. Alendouro Pinho, Henrique P. Veiga, Alessandro Veneri, Daniel Passos, Ivo Souza, Bruno Amorim, Jo\~ao M. Viana Parente Lopes, Aires Ferreira

- Real‑space linear‑response theory for orbital magnetization induced by a dc electric field in non‑translationally invariant bulk solids.
- Linear‑in‑wavevector conductivity expressed via single‑particle Green’s functions, velocity and position operators, yielding a gauge‑invariant real‑space formulation.
- Recovers Bloch‑picture results in the translationally invariant limit; demonstrated on a disordered Haldane model with large‑scale Chebyshev expansions.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23404)

### Local spin constraints and symmetry in single-$Q$ spiral magnets

**Authors:** Lasin Thaivalappil, Shin-Ming Huang

- Constrained Luttinger–Tisza formulation enforces local spin‑length, reducing single‑sublattice minimization to a secular equation for the spiral‑plane normal and two‑sublattice minimization to a frame‑independent energy on two unit normals.
- Symmetry analysis distinguishes operations that preserve or reverse the Fourier arm and those that preserve or exchange sublattices, yielding explicit constraints on allowed spiral states.
- Numerical tests show that enforcing local circularity lowers the energy and can shift the preferred ordering wave vector relative to the unconstrained LT solution in both symmetric and anisotropic two‑sublattice models.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23882)

### Stabilizer models of achiral abelian topological orders

**Authors:** Marvin Qi, Wilbur Shirley

- Continuous‑variable stabilizer codes realize every achiral 2D abelian topological order (bosonic and fermionic).
- Any signature‑zero integer \(K\)-matrix order is obtained by condensing a lattice of dyons with trivial mutual braiding in an \(\mathbb{R}^N\) gauge theory.
- The condensation is implemented microscopically by gauging a non‑on‑site 1‑form symmetry of the \(\mathbb{R}^N\) toric code.
