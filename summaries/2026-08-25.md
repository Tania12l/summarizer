### Criticality in Neural Network Function Space through Wilsonian Fixed Points and Finite-Width Corrections

**Authors:** Eric Howard, Iftekher S. Chowdhury, Hardique Dasore, Hom Nath Dhungana

- Infinite-width nets ↔ free-field fixed point; finite width adds non‑Gaussian interactions via Wilsonian RG.

- Width, depth, activation, init variance, and training dynamics serve as control parameters; overparameterization suppresses interactions, exposing finite‑width critical structure.

- The revised model matches analytical benchmarks and reproduces shear‑dominated fractures in hydraulic fracturing and biaxial compression simulations.

### Conservation capacity of local learning rules in physical networks

**Authors:** Bijaya Dangol

- Local learning rules in resistive networks conserve a “mass‑like” function tied to each circuit sector, determined by Tellegen’s identity and Kirchhoff’s laws.
- The number of independent conserved masses equals a topological property bounded by the number of output electrodes, yielding a capacity theory for memory protection.
- In linear circuits, these conserved identities are exactly classified, with adjoint coupled learning draining sectors at rates set by squared output multipliers.

### Distinguishing Mott and strain-induced anisotropic semimetals in magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene using Landau level spectroscopy

**Authors:** Keshav Singh, Juan Felipe Mendez-Valderrama, Erez Berg, B. Andrei Bernevig, Oskar Vafek

### Electronic Toroidal Metals: Landau Theory and Magnetoelectric Fingerprints

**Authors:** Yuhang Xiao, Ning Hao

- Introduces electronic toroidal metal (ETM) as a Fermi‑liquid instability with toroidal order emerging from itinerant electrons, using a projected criterion that removes uniform charge‑current contributions.
- Defines the toroidal moment through the antisymmetric magnetoelectric tensor, linking it to pseudospin‑vortex topology and showing that changes in vortex winding markedly enhance the response.
- Predicts distinctive nonlinear charge transport in ETM, including a pronounced interband quantum‑geometric contribution.
