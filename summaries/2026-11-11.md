### Goldstone-Mediated Chiral Superconductivity in Self-Doped Electron Crystals

**Authors:** Shuchen Zhang, Zhihuan Dong, Zhaoyu Han, Shubhayu Chatterjee

- Goldstone phonons from self‑doped electron crystals can overscreen Coulomb repulsion, enabling chiral topological superconductivity.
- The mechanism operates near the trivial–topological crystal boundary, producing a small Dirac Fermi sea that couples to phonons.
- Predictions include a single Majorana edge mode and distinctive nonlinear I‑V signatures testable in rhombohedral graphene.

### Borrowed Planckian dissipation in magic-angle twisted graphene

**Authors:** Dino \v{S}krgi\'c, Dejan Stojkovic

- Displacement field \(D\) in magic‑angle twisted trilayer graphene breaks mirror symmetry, causing Dirac carriers to inherit Planckian self‑energy from flat bands with weight \(\propto D^2+D_0^2\).
- Resistivity remains linear in \(T\) with slope \(a(1+D^2/D_0^\), showing the field tunes scattering rate rather than carrier density.
- Borrowed dissipation keeps flat‑band transport prefactor and entropy, giving a field‑differential ratio ≈ 46 for a conformal flat band.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12947)

### Hidden Spin-Supercurrent Counterflow in Conventional Superconductors

**Authors:** Johanne Bratland Tjernshaugen, Jacob Linder

- Spin‑splitting in a conventional superconductor allows resistive spin currents to vary spatially while the total spin current remains conserved via a counterflow of condensate spin current.
- Spin injection through a spin‑mixing interface produces opposite resistive and condensate spin currents, yielding a hidden counterflow with zero net spin and charge.
- This counterflow survives over broad parameter ranges and can be detected through spin‑to‑charge conversion (e.g., spin‑Hall or inverse spin‑Hall signals).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13076)

### Chiral Superconductivity Indifferent to Parent Topology in the Square-Lattice Hofstadter-Hubbard Model

**Authors:** Jia-Xin Zhang, Wen O. Wang, Leon Balents, Lucile Savary, D. N. Sheng

- DMRG and parton analysis uncover IQH, CSL, and AFM phases in a π‑flux square‑lattice Hofstadter‑Hubbard model at half filling.
- Finite‑T DQMC shows a closing charge gap but an open spin gap at the IQH–CSL boundary, signaling topological criticality.
- Doping any of these phases produces a robust chiral superconductor with spin Chern number Cs = –2, regardless of the parent state.

### Quantum Control Geometry as a Metric of Chaos

**Authors:** Mahmoud Abdelshafy, Joaquin F. Rodriguez-Nieva

- Introduces a quantum geometric susceptibility metric that quantifies eigenstate sensitivity to physically allowed Hamiltonian deformations, offering a continuous measure of chaos.
- Finds that minimal susceptibility aligns with maximal eigenstate randomness, broad local operator spectra, and suppression of nonhydrodynamic slow modes in canonical chaotic models.
- Provides an intrinsic, unifying framework linking eigenstate structure to dynamics, enabling comparative assessment of chaos across many‑body systems.

### Thermodynamic Properties of the S=1/2 Zigzag Spin Ladder

**Authors:** Nobuya Maeshima, Toshiya Hikihara, Kouichi Okunishi

- Finite‑temperature properties of a spin‑½ zigzag ladder with anisotropic Γ interaction are studied using iTEBD+purification and exact diagonalization.
