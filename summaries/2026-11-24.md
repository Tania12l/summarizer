### Ground-State Manifold Curvature and Phase Transitions in the Kitaev Honeycomb Model

**Authors:** Omid Yahyayi Monem, Setare Bashari, Hosein Mohammadzadeh

- Ricci scalar of the quantum metric diverges negatively at critical lines between the three gapped phases, with closed‑form expressions related by coordinate changes.
- In the gapless phase the curvature is positive, decays as \(1/\ln L\), diverges at boundaries with a distinct exponent, and a magnetic field introduces a finite IR cutoff yielding positive curvature and a quadratic‑width crossover.
- The divergence of the Ricci scalar thus serves as a geometric signature of all phase transitions in the Kitaev honeycomb model.
