### Stochastic Diversity Stabilization in Nonlinear Competitive Dynamics: A Statistical Mechanics Approach to Fluctuation Suppression and Finite-Size Scaling

**Authors:** Ruocun Ou

- Noise intensity needed to maintain diversity scales as σ_c ∼ N^(-α), with α ≈ (γ+/2 depending on the nonlinearity γ.

### Resonant-pair energy return beyond the early Green-Kubo plateau in a quartic chain

**Authors:** Julian Olszewski, Zijian Liu, Jie Xu

- Resonant neighbor exchange in a weakly coupled quartic chain produces coherent energy return, reshaping the early Green–Kubo plateau.
- The pair heat‑current integral maps to a classical pendulum, predicting a negative first minimum at 952 T_lib with depth –74 × the independent‑cell exchange coefficient.
- Chaotic cells or energy‑conserving noise suppress or interrupt this coherent return, offering a quantitative benchmark to separate early exchange from later correlated dynamics.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30172)

### Vacancy-driven topological transition of a Chern insulator

**Authors:** Peize Ding, Raquel Queiroz

- Microscopic model of strong‑disorder transition in a Chern insulator via in‑gap ring states bound to vacancies.
- Periodic vacancy lattice produces a Dirac inversion of the zero‑energy kernel, changing the Chern number; disorder handled with SCBA, transfer‑matrix, and multifractal scaling.
- Local Chern marker and current textures track the real‑space transition, showing nonperturbative ring states as the key microscopic degrees of freedom.

### Nature of the bosonic edge phase in a double quantum spin Hall insulator

**Authors:** Andreas Feuerpfeil, Ronny Thomale, Trithep Devakul, Nicolas Regnault, Julian May-Mann

- Demonstrated numerically that a double quantum spin Hall insulator edge can transition to a bosonic phase where single‑fermion excitations are gapped but fermion pairs remain gapless.
- Used segment DMRG on an infinite cylinder embedding the bulk to capture the mixed charge‑\(S^z\) anomaly and confirmed the predicted scaling of the single‑fermion gap with interaction strength.
- Discussed experimental relevance to twisted homobilayers of WSe₂/MoTe₂ and extended the segment‑geometry method to other topological phases with edge anomalies.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30251)

### Discrete time liquids

**Authors:** Zuo Wang, Paul M. Schindler, Marin Bukov, Marios H. Michael

- Discrete time liquids: metastable period‑doubled dynamics with only short‑range temporal coherence.
- Time‑solitons—topological defects in time—cause stochastic switching between symmetry‑related temporal states, destroying long‑range coherence.
- A minimal model reproduces the temporal correlation distribution, describing the time liquid as a time crystal with a finite density of time‑solitons.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30266)

### Charge-monitored Luttinger liquid

**Authors:** Victor Gurarie, Leo Radzihovsky

- Area‑law vs. volume‑law phases arise from competition between weak continuous density measurements and particle tunneling in non‑conserving Luttinger liquids.
- The volume‑law phase is always charge‑fuzzy, and the phase boundary follows a universal dependence of the Luttinger parameter on measurement rate.
- This universal scaling contrasts with the non‑universal phase boundaries seen in generic highly excited 1D systems outside the Luttinger liquid regime.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30271)

### Charge Density Waves and In-Gap States in Coupled Chains and Moir\'e Superlattices

**Authors:** Muhammad Akram, Onur Erten

- Coupled SSH chains show a sequence of CDW phases (dimers, trimers, incommensurate) controlled by electron‑phonon coupling versus interchain tunneling.
- The CDWs are gapped and host symmetry‑protected bound states pinned by crystalline symmetries, not at zero energy.
- Lattice mismatch forms long‑period super‑moiré CDWs with defect arrays that localize electronic states, enabling tunable control via interchain tunneling and mismatch.

### ${\pi}$-Flux Tuning of Quantum Paramagnetism in the Triangular-Lattice Hubbard Model

**Authors:** Zhaochen Liu, Andreas Feuerpfeil, Ronny Thomale

- Zero‑flux vs staggered π‑flux triangular‑lattice Hubbard models at half filling.
- Positive ring exchange in zero‑flux → chiral spin liquid; negative ring exchange in π‑flux → no scalar chirality.
- Flux‑tuned ring exchange efficiently distinguishes chiral from non‑chiral quantum paramagnets at finite coupling.

**Authors:** Ao Chen, Johnnie Gray, Si-Jing Du, Garnet Kin-Lic Chan

### Nonlinear Canonical Transformations for Studying Spin-1/2 Hamiltonians

**Authors:** Swarnamoy Ghosh, Thomas M. Henderson, Gustavo E. Scuseria

- Introduces a nonlinear spin‑fermion‑spin (SFS) transformation that maps strongly correlated spin Hamiltonians to a form where mean‑field ground states are more accurate.
- Shows that the transformed Hamiltonian can be treated with broken‑symmetry mean‑field theory and post‑mean‑field methods, reducing energy errors for XXZ and \(J_1\)-\(J_2\) models.
- Demonstrates that spin‑BCS coupled‑cluster calculations in the transformed basis achieve substantially better accuracy than conventional spin‑BCS approaches.

### Anisotropic thermoelectric transport in the Sr2RuO4-Ru eutectic system

**Authors:** Takeru Ito, Shogo Yoshida, Hiroshi Yaguchi, Ryuji Okazaki

- In-plane thermopower of Sr₂RuO₄–Ru eutectic shows strong anisotropy between gradients parallel and perpendicular to Ru lamellae.
- Finite‑element strain modeling and transport calculations reveal that uniaxial compression of Sr₂RuO₄ alters its Fermi surfaces, generating the observed thermopower anisotropy.
- Strain‑induced electronic‑structure changes at the Sr₂RuO₄–Ru interfaces dominate the effect, with possible additional contributions from interfacial mixing and 3‑D connectivity.

### String Order in Quench Dynamics as a Probe of Quantum Phase Transitions

**Authors:** H. P. Zhang, Z. Song

- Finds that both equilibrium and long‑time quench averages of the order parameter diverge at Ising critical lines and detect anisotropy transitions.

### Impact of localized $4f$ magnetism on Berry curvature and the intrinsic anomalous Hall effect in CeRu$_2$Si$_2$

**Authors:** Takehiro Kobayashi, Shunsuke Hirai, Ibuki Terada, Michi-To Suzuki

- Intrinsic AHC stays finite in CeRu₂Si₂ even when Ce 4f states are localized and absent from the Fermi surface.
- AHC magnitude depends on the occupied Ce 4f state, as exchange splitting of conduction bands is induced.
- Localized 4f magnetism creates Berry curvature near the Fermi surface, allowing nonmagnetic conduction bands to contribute to a large intrinsic AHE.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30684)
