### Dynamical Tunneling Blockade in Layer-Pseudospin Ferromagnets

**Authors:** Lingxian Kong, Yuta Murakami, Ryuichi Shindou, Zhiyuan Sun

- Low bias: static state with parallel valley pseudospins; high bias: layer‑spin precession locks valleys antiparallel.
- Dynamical tunneling blockade suppresses interlayer tunneling, eliminating ac current and sharply reducing dc current.
- Contrasts with conventional quantum Hall double layers, revealing a new nonequilibrium regime in flat‑band systems.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28760)

### Theory of the intrinsic optical anomalous Hall responses in time-reversal symmetry breaking superconductors

**Authors:** Jia-Long Zhang, Wen Huang

- Unified theory of intrinsic optical Hall effect in TRSB multiband superconductors, linking the response to quantum‑geometric interband velocity (Berry connection).
- Distinguishes unitary vs nonunitary TRSB states: interband pairing is required for unitary Hall response, while nonunitary states can exhibit Hall via single‑electron tunneling if spin‑orbit coupling is present.
- Identifies two tunneling channels—single‑electron and Cooper‑pair—driven by interband processes, and derives an effective theory by integrating out hole degrees of freedom.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28786)

### Two quantum skyrmions form a magnon Josephson junction

**Authors:** R. L. Silva

- Two quantum skyrmions bind into a molecule that behaves as a magnon Josephson junction, \(H=E_C n^2-W\cos\theta\).
- DMRG on the \(J_1\)–\(J_2\) triangular lattice gives \(E_C\) from the energy curvature at seven flipped spins and \(W\simeq02J_1\), half the skyrmion–antiskyrmion tunneling splitting, independent of anisotropy.
- The helicity qubits on either side are strongly coupled through this intrinsic interaction, offering a new qubit architecture.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28854)

**Authors:** Justin Tahmassebpur, Chia-Hao Lee, Cristob\'al M\'endez, Shake Karapetyan, Schuyler Z. Shi, David A. Muller, Tom\'as Arias

- Introduces Hereditary Effective Atom Theory (HEAT), a genetic algorithm with differentiable effective‑atom representation for large‑scale structure prediction.

**Authors:** Dominik Krengel, Jian Chen, Shun Nomura, Hidenori Takahashi

- Peak and steady‑state drag of a pulled plate depend on depth and inter‑particle friction.
- High friction lets wall forces boost drag until failure; steady state redirects chains upward, reducing wall effects.
- Low friction lets overburden pressure dominate at deep depths, suppressing dilation and slowing the decay of steady‑state drag.

### Interplay of disorder and phonons in two-dimensional transport

**Authors:** Aryaman Babbar, Sankar Das Sarma

- Bilayer MoSe₂ resistivity is governed mainly by screened charged impurities and neutral defects; acoustic phonons give only a minor correction.
- A resistivity minimum appears on both metallic and insulating sides: on the insulating side from activated carriers, on the metallic side from disorder–phonon interplay.
- Boltzmann transport with RPA screening reproduces experimental temperature‑ and density‑dependent data, confirming phonons are negligible over the measured range.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29260)

### Ab Initio Approach for Quantum Magnets with Coupled Cluster and Transcorrelation

**Authors:** Kristoffer Simula, Nikolay A. Bogdanov, Olle Gunnarsson, Daniel Kats, Ali Alavi

- Ab initio determination of nearest‑neighbour exchange \(J_1\) in five oxygen‑bridged spin‑½ quantum magnets using embedded‑cluster 2‑determinant CCSD.
- Basis‑set errors are large; CBS extrapolation shifts \(J_1\) by up to 50 meV, while transcorrelated Hamiltonians recover CBS accuracy with modest basis sizes.
- First demonstration of transcorrelated methods in strongly correlated solids, showing high accuracy and affordable cost for magnetic coupling predictions.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29324)

### Chiral-Nematic Lattice Instability in Charge Density Wave of 1T-TiSe2

**Authors:** Abdurrafi Ahsan Alghifari, Euihwan Do, Jae Whan Park, Han Woong Yeom

- Ab initio calculations reveal a chiral monoclinic (CCDW in 1T‑TiSe₂ stabilized by cooperative phonon condensation, reproducing the observed energy gap and topography.
- The potential is exceptionally soft along the symmetry‑breaking direction, allowing the lattice to fluctuate through fully asymmetric configurations while preserving an average chiral structure.
- This dynamical chiral CDW reconciles chiral domains seen in imaging with symmetry loss in bulk spectroscopy, highlighting lattice instability and fluctuations as the microscopic origin of chirality.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29448)

### On thermal Hall effect in boson systems

**Authors:** Yu. V. Baramygina, D. N. Aristov

- Investigates thermal Hall effect (THE) in bosonic magnon systems, especially skyrmion lattice insulators.
- Derives new temperature‑dependent formulas, revealing a finite 1/T residual term when anomalous magnon interactions are present.
- Suggests THE can detect topological transitions in magnon spectra and may mimic a Meissner‑like effect at low temperatures.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29482)
