### Kelvin-Helmholtz instability in annular superfluids across the UFG-BCS crossover

**Authors:** Micha{\l} \'Sliwi\'nski, Klejdja Xhani, Gabriel Wlaz{\l}owski

- Studied Kelvin‑Helmholtz instability in counter‑rotating annular Fermi superfluids across the UFG‑BCS crossover with time‑dependent superfluid density functional theory.

- In deep BCS regime, boundary‑generated antivortices proliferate faster than the instability, destroying the vortex necklace before full development.

### Symmetry-selective nonrelativistic spin splitting in antiferromagnets driven by coherent phonons

**Authors:** Sangeeta Rajpurohit, Mohsen Yarmohammadi, Sheikh Rubaiat Ul Haque, Tony F. Heinz, Aaron M. Lindenberg, Tadashi Ogitsu

- Coherent infrared‑active phonons lift spin degeneracy in collinear antiferromagnets, creating nonrelativistic spin splitting (NRSS) without magnetization.
- The symmetry of the excited phonon determines the spin‑split phase: a mode breaking all sublattice‑connecting symmetries yields an s‑wave NRSS; a mode preserving a mirror symmetry produces a d‑wave altermagnetic state.
- Spin splitting grows linearly with phonon amplitude and reverses when the lattice displacement is inverted, enabling polarization‑ and mode‑selective ultrafast control.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01844)

### Thermodynamic Electric Toroidal Dipole and Intrinsic Longitudinal Spin Transport

**Authors:** Taisei Yamanaka, Takumi Sato, Satoru Hayami

- Defines a gauge‑invariant thermodynamic electric toroidal dipole (ETD) as a bulk ferroaxial order parameter in periodic crystals.
- Proves that the chemical‑potential derivative of the ETD equals the intrinsic longitudinal spin conductivity, linking ferroaxial order to spin transport.
- Validates the theory with a ferroaxial Kane–Mele model, where the ETD appears only in the ferroaxial phase, is amplified near a small band gap, and drives a sizable longitudinal spin current.
