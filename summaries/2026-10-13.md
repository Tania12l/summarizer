### Quantum-magneto oscillations in a supramolecular Mn(II)-[3 x 3] grid

**Authors:** O. Waldmann, S. Carretta, P. Santini, R. Koch, A. G. M. Jansen, G. Amoretti, R. Caciuffo, L. Zhao, L. K. Thompson

- High‑field torque magnetometry on Mn(II)-[3 × 3] grid reveals oscillatory torque vs. field above 5 T.
- Oscillations observed at 3 He temperatures, indicating quantum‑magneto effects.
- A theoretical model successfully reproduces the magneto‑oscillation behavior.

### Ferromagnetism in Fe-doped SnO2 thin films

**Authors:** J. M. D. Coey, A. P. Douvalis, C. B. Fitzgerald, M. Venkatesan

- Fe‑doped SnO₂ thin films are transparent ferromagnets with TC ≈ 610 K and Ms ≈ 2 Am²/kg.
- Mossbauer spectra reveal high‑spin Fe³⁺; only ~23 % of Fe ions are magnetically ordered, each carrying ~8 µB.
- Ferromagnetism attributed to Fe³⁺ coupling through an electron trapped in an oxygen vacancy (F‑center).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401293v1)

### Phases of a rotating Bose-Einstein condensate with anharmonic confinement

**Authors:** A. D. Jackson, G. M. Kavoulakis, E. Lundh

- Identified three phases (multiple, single, mixed quantization) in a rotating BEC with quadratic‑plus‑quartic confinement.
- Derived a universal phase diagram; continuous transitions exact for weak coupling and small anharmonicity.
- Variational predictions agree with Gross‑Pitaevskii numerical solutions.

### Observation of narrow fluorescence from doubly driven four-level atoms at room temperature

**Authors:** Uday Kumar Khan, Jimmy Sebastian, N. Kamaraju, Andal Narayanan, R. Srinivasan, Hema Ramachandran

- Narrow fluorescence peaks appear in Rb‑85 under dual‑laser 3D molasses; one laser fixed detuned from cooling transition, the other scanned over repumping lines.
- Peaks form symmetric pairs whose separation increases with cooling‑laser detuning; large detunings yield additional small peaks.
- A simple model explains the observed spectral features.

### Vortex waves and the onset of turbulence in $^3$He-B

**Authors:** K. L. Henderson, C. F. Barenghi

- Finne et al. identified the intrinsic turbulence onset criterion \(q=\alpha/(1-\alpha')<1\) in \(^{3}\)He‑B.
- The criterion corresponds to marginally damped Kelvin waves; for \(q>1\) waves cannot grow to trigger reconnections.
- Analysis of rotating‑superfluid modes confirms \(q=1\) as the crossover between damped and propagating Kelvin waves in the long‑wavelength limit.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401212v1)

### A new approach to the Kondo effect: sophomore's dream function and Lee-Yang zeros

**Authors:** V. Yu. Irkhin

- Uses a sophomore’s dream–based partition function to map the Kondo crossover onto a ladder of equidistant Lee‑Yang zeros.
- Summing the ladder gives a universal Gamma‑function backbone Γ(α+T_K/(2πT)) with α = ½ set by the Friedel sum rule.
- Derives a compact, one‑parameter Fano‑lineshape crossover formula, extendable to SU(N) Coqblin‑Schrieffer, underscreened, Anderson, and lattice Kondo models.

### Ultrafast laser-stimulated field emission from a metal: a non-equilibrium Green's functions approach

**Authors:** Luciano Jacopo D'Onofrio, Adolfo Avella, Martin Eckstein

- Developed a non‑equilibrium Green’s function framework that incorporates strong electron‑electron correlations for ultrafast laser‑stimulated field emission from metallic nanotips.
- Demonstrated that emission spectra result from filtering the emitter’s local density of states through field‑dressed vacuum propagators, covering both strong‑field and multi‑photon regimes.
- Uncovered a dynamic back‑action regime where optical‑field‑induced periodic polarization reshapes the far‑field spectrum via self‑consistent Dyson equation solutions.

### Zak Phase Quantization without Global Symmetry through Destructive Interference

**Authors:** Geonwoo Hwang, Jun-Won Rhim

- Zak phase remains quantized via destructive interference that localizes Bloch states to a topological parent, even when global symmetry is broken.
- Verified in an inversion‑broken 1D model and a reflection‑broken armchair nanoribbon, preserving π Zak phase and edge states.
- Perturbations breaking the interference continuously dequantize the Zak phase, though a near‑π regime with boundary response can persist.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13250v1)

### Percolation of Data Constraints and Reorganization of Boolean Function States in Parity Learning

**Authors:** Han Ma

- Neural nets reorganize Boolean function states as parity data grow, measured by inverse subsystem purity (effective Schmidt rank).
- Small data additions broaden the Schmidt spectrum (differentiation); larger additions reconcentrate it (target‑selective recoherence), boosting target alignment and accuracy.
- Learning unfolds in three stages—constraint identifiability, function‑state organization, predictive accuracy—allowing re‑differentiation (retreat) without changing graph connectivity.
