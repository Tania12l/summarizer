### Shapiro spikes in driven quantum fluids

**Authors:** Vijay Pal Singh, Ludwig Mathey, Luigi Amico

- Shapiro spikes appear in a neutral atomic Josephson junction, with dc‑current resonances scaling as Bessel functions of drive strength.
- Phase‑winding locks to integer multiples of the drive frequency through quantized‑vortex nucleation and 2π phase slips, creating plateaus.
- In a locking plateau the dc current and density transfer can reverse direction (negative mobility) while the phase‑winding remains locked.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05477)

### Quasicrystals are Functionally Graded Materials

**Authors:** Michael J. Landry, Dominic Bollettieri, Akhil Sadam, Denisse C\'ordova Carrizales, Mingda Li

- Quasicrystals mapped exactly to periodic approximants with spatially graded phason, linking them to functionally graded materials.
- Introduced a quantum‑metric‑based control parameter that dictates when finite approximations are reliable.
- Built a Hamiltonian that predicts Bragg gaps, quasimomentum equivalence, and quasiperiodic eigenstates to all orders in phason strain, verified numerically and testable via diffraction.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05478)

### Resonantly interacting $2e/3$ anyons: a controlled microscopic theory of anyon superconductivity

**Authors:** Qingchen Li, Pavel A. Nosov, Zhaoyu Han, Si-Yuan Chen, Taige Wang, Eslam Khalaf

- Microscopic theory connects lightly doped Laughlin state to a dilute gas of charge‑\(2e/3\) anyons via geometric quantization.
- Effective Hamiltonian includes valley structure, dispersion, statistical and short‑range interactions; ground state is exactly solvable in an Aharonov‑Casher framework.
- Shows Cooper‑pair off‑diagonal long‑range order in electron coordinates, identifies pairing symmetry and vortex lattice, and pinpoints relevant detuning and marginal three‑body contact perturbations controlling compressibility and sound velocity.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05529)

### Bypass Transition via the Non-Hermitian Skin Effect

**Authors:** Konghao Sun, Haiping Hu

- Non‑Hermitian skin lattices amplify signals directionally, enabling a bypass transition to self‑sustained motion from a finite local excitation while the resting state remains linearly stable.
- Return‑map theory predicts an exponentially decreasing triggering threshold with chain length and scaling with directional gain; weak noise can ignite motion with a logarithmically growing ignition length.
- The work establishes a skin‑effect route to bypass transitions, offering a new framework for predicting and controlling nonlinear wave dynamics.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05563)

### Exact Mirror-Parity Selection Rule for Electron-Phonon Coupling in Two-Dimensional Crystals

**Authors:** Nicol\'as F. Barrera, Javiera Cabezas-Escares, Francisco Munoz, Carlos Cardenas, Julia Contreras-Garc\'ia

- Mirror‑parity rule: \(g_{mn,\nu}(\mathbf{k},\mathbf{q})\neq0\) only if \(s_n(\mathbf{k})\,\chi_\nu(\mathbf{q})\,s_m(\mathbf{k}+\mathbf{q})=+1\).
- Even phonons couple same‑parity states (even–even or odd–odd); odd phonons couple opposite‑parity states (even–odd).
- First‑principles test on 2D superconductor Sc₂BCH₄ shows symmetry‑forbidden matrix elements vanish to numerical precision.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05710)

### Interplay between Spin Stripes and Pairing Crossover in the Bilayer Hubbard Model

**Authors:** Zixuan Jia, Xuelei Sui, Shijie Hu, Tianxing Ma, Haiqing Lin, Bing Huang

- Spin stripes appear with hole doping, triggering a d‑wave → s±‑wave pairing crossover.
- The crossover follows the shift of magnetic correlations: interlayer AFM overtakes intralayer AFM.
- Momentum‑dependent reorganization of the spin structure factor signals fluctuating spin stripes tied to pairing.

### Field evolution of the multi-$k$ magnetic states in NdB$_4$ revealed by neutron diffraction

**Authors:** D. Brunt, N. Qureshi, E. Chan, B. Ouladdiaf, G. Balakrishnan, D. D. Khalyavin, O. A. Petrenko

- NdB₄ exhibits a 1/5‑magnetization plateau at ~12 kOe, marked by a shift of propagation vectors from (1/5,0,–2//(2/5,0,1/to (0,0,1//(0,0,2/.
- At ~33 kOe the plateau collapses; non‑integer‑k reflections vanish while k = 0 reflections remain, indicating incomplete field‑polarization.
- Data support two coupled instabilities: in‑plane all‑in‑all‑out order and an out‑of‑plane spin‑density wave.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06526)

### Stability of energy gap of macroscopic quantum matter

**Authors:** Kangle Li, Liujun Zou

- Shows gapped local Hamiltonians can become gapless under arbitrarily weak local perturbations in the thermodynamic limit.
- Proves that in 1D unstable gaps require broken translation invariance and fine‑tuning; in higher dimensions fine‑tuned, translation‑invariant Hamiltonians can also be unstable.
- Highlights implications for phase stability, anyon superconductivity, and higher‑form symmetries.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06528)

### Hidden entropic order of emergent charges in a classical Amp\`ere spin liquid

**Authors:** H. Diego Rosales, Emma M. Bizot, Flavia A. G\'omez Albarrac\'in, Pierre Pujol

- Ising Ampère spin liquid on octochlore lattice: ground states obey zero‑circulation Gauss law, predicting pinch‑point correlations.
- Maximal charges self‑organize on one sublattice with random signs, forming an entropic sublattice order that erases pinch points and produces no magnetic Bragg peaks.
- The transition to this hidden order is continuous, in the 3D Ising universality class, while preserving extensive entropy and time‑reversal symmetry.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06795)

### Noise-Induced Topology in Chiral Quantum Walks

**Authors:** Alexandre Chaduteau, Derek K. K. Lee, Frank Schindler, Abhinav Prem

- Dephasing noise turns chiral asymmetry into a topologically protected skewness, with density profile skew growing while the center remains fixed.
- Noise induces a non‑Hermitian skin effect: late‑time modes pile up at one edge without drift, and dephasing can trigger a topological phase transition that reverses the asymmetry.
- The noise‑averaged dynamics is an interacting Lindbladian whose bound‑state band has a point gap; its winding number fixes the asymmetry direction and enables a quantum ratchet on noisy flux ladders.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05471)

### Rotated-Frame Determinant Discovery for Ground- and Excited-State Quantum Selected Configuration Interaction

**Authors:** Yukihiro Okuno

- RSU samples a state in complementary orbital frames, rotating observed determinants back to the canonical basis to efficiently build a selected‑CI space.
- RSU is paired with GQE for excited‑state targeting; candidate circuits are classically vetted via selected‑CI diagonalization, requiring only state preparation and sampling on the quantum device.
- For stretched N₂, RSU reaches chemical accuracy with 5,000 shots versus 20,000 for conventional QSCI, and similar improvements hold for approximate GQE states and excited states.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05941)
