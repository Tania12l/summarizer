### Analytical Solution of the Nonlinear Boltzmann Equation For Multicomponent Systems

**Authors:** Linyuan Wei, Yi Wang, Jin Hu, Baoyi Chen

- Exact analytical solutions of the nonlinear relativistic Boltzmann equation for homogeneous, isotropic massless multicomponent systems with anisotropic scattering.
- For two-component systems, three solution classes: equilibrium, a hybrid Maxwell‑Jüttner plus nontrivial distribution, and shared nontrivial dynamics.
- The hybrid Maxwell‑Jüttner structure and shared nontrivial dynamics are unprecedented in kinetic theory and relativistic contexts.
