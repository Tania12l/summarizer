### FrOGS: Discrete Neural Sampler for Independent Alloy Configurations Across Chemical Conditions

### Resolving the Magnetic Ground-State Controversy in RuO2 through A Flat Magnetic Energy Landscape

**Authors:** Tianxiao Liang, Fanhan Kong, Jijun Zhao, Xue Jiang

- RuO₂ exhibits an exceptionally flat magnetic energy landscape where nonmagnetic and multiple altermagnetic states are nearly degenerate.
- Material perturbations (e.g., defects) select the ground state mainly by altering localized Heisenberg exchange, with secondary Stoner polarization contributions.
- Spin‑orbit coupling fixes the Néel vector orientation but does not drive the emergence of long‑range magnetic order.
