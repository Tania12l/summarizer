### Quantum-magneto oscillations in a supramolecular Mn(II)-[3 x 3] grid

**Authors:** O. Waldmann, S. Carretta, P. Santini, R. Koch, A. G. M. Jansen, G. Amoretti, R. Caciuffo, L. Zhao, L. K. Thompson

- High‑field torque magnetometry on Mn(II)-[3 × 3] grid reveals oscillatory torque vs. field above 5 T.
- Oscillations observed at 3 He temperatures, indicating quantum‑magneto effects.
- A theoretical model successfully reproduces the magneto‑oscillation behavior.

### Ferromagnetism in Fe-doped SnO2 thin films

**Authors:** J. M. D. Coey, A. P. Douvalis, C. B. Fitzgerald, M. Venkatesan

- Fe‑doped SnO₂ thin films are transparent ferromagnets with TC ≈ 610 K and Ms ≈ 2 Am²/kg.
- Mossbauer spectra reveal high‑spin Fe³⁺; only ~23 % of Fe ions are magnetically ordered, each carrying ~8 µB.
- Ferromagnetism attributed to Fe³⁺ coupling through an electron trapped in an oxygen vacancy (F‑center).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401293v1)

**Authors:** V. K. Thorsmolle, R. D. Averitt, X. Chi, D. J. Hilton, D. L. Smith, A. P. Ramirez, A. J. Taylor

**Authors:** A. D. Jackson, G. M. Kavoulakis, E. Lundh

### Observation of narrow fluorescence from doubly driven four-level atoms at room temperature

**Authors:** Uday Kumar Khan, Jimmy Sebastian, N. Kamaraju, Andal Narayanan, R. Srinivasan, Hema Ramachandran

- Narrow fluorescence peaks appear in Rb‑85 under dual‑laser 3D molasses; one laser fixed detuned from cooling transition, the other scanned over repumping lines.
- Peaks form symmetric pairs whose separation increases with cooling‑laser detuning; large detunings yield additional small peaks.
- A simple model explains the observed spectral features.

### Origin of asymmetric reversal modes in ferromagnetic/antiferromagnetic multilayers

**Authors:** B. Beckmann, U. Nowak, K. D. Usadel

- Asymmetric reversal modes appear in certain exchange‑bias multilayers.
- Domain‑state simulations show the effect depends on the angle between the AFM easy axis and the applied field.
- At specific angles the FM reverses symmetrically via coherent rotation; at others it reverses asymmetrically, sometimes producing zero perpendicular magnetization.

### Vortex waves and the onset of turbulence in $^3$He-B

**Authors:** K. L. Henderson, C. F. Barenghi

- Finne et al. identified the intrinsic turbulence onset criterion \(q=\alpha/(1-\alpha')<1\) in \(^{3}\)He‑B.
- The criterion corresponds to marginally damped Kelvin waves; for \(q>1\) waves cannot grow to trigger reconnections.
- Analysis of rotating‑superfluid modes confirms \(q=1\) as the crossover between damped and propagating Kelvin waves in the long‑wavelength limit.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401212v1)

### Dimensional Crossover and Correlated Spin Dynamics in the Chain-Like van der Waals Antiferromagnet AgCrP$_2$S$_6$

**Authors:** C. S. Saramgi, Y. Shemerliuk, S. Selter, S. Aswartham, B. Buechner, H. -J. Grafe, K. M. Ranjith

- Quasi‑1D S=3/2 antiferromagnet with intrachain exchange J≈100–140 K, seen in a broad susceptibility maximum at ~250 K.
- 31P NMR shows two K–χ regimes near 250 K, T^5 relaxation below TN, and a critical exponent β≈36, indicating 3D ordering.
- Dimensional crossover: strong 1D short‑range correlations above TN evolve into 3D antiferromagnetic order within the layered van der Waals host.

### Particle-hole asymmetry and a fluctuating pair-density-wave state in a lightly doped Mott insulator on the square lattice

**Authors:** Hao-Xin Wang, Zheng-Yuan Yue, Wen-Yuan Liu, Zheng-Cheng Gu

- Electron‑like \(t'>0\) yields robust, nearly uniform \(d\)-wave pairing; decreasing \(t'\) suppresses pairing.
- As \(t'\) becomes hole‑like, a period‑two plaquette modulation appears, evolving into a charge‑dominated checkerboard with period ≈ 4 \(a_0\).
- DMRG and PEPS show that in the hole‑like regime pair correlations peak at \((\pi,,(0,\pi)\) and decay within a few sites, while CDW dominates.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14101v1)

### Landau Theory of Black-Brane Phase Transitions from Kasner Singularities

**Authors:** Hong-Da Lyu

- Landau theory for black‑brane transition built from interior geometry: normal phase has Reissner–Nordström Cauchy horizon, scalarized phase ends in a Kasner singularity.
- Conserved radial charge yields grand potential from either endpoint; quadratic term vanishes at criticality, allowing a Landau potential whose coefficients come solely from the terminal singularity.
- Theory predicts critical scaling of the Kasner order parameter and grand‑potential difference, agreeing with full backreacted numerics for two scalar self‑interaction models.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14075v1)

- Demonstrated anomalous Hall crystals in hole‑doped moiré rhombohedral graphene through transport and broadband noise measurements.

### Thermodynamic Properties of the S=1/2 Zigzag Spin Ladder

**Authors:** Nobuya Maeshima, Toshiya Hikihara, Kouichi Okunishi

- Finite‑temperature properties of a spin‑½ zigzag ladder with anisotropic Γ interaction are studied using iTEBD+purification and exact diagonalization.
- Entropy is suppressed in gapped phases but enhanced near phase boundaries; the specific‑heat gap matches the one‑spinon gap, while ED yields a two‑spinon gap.
- At J₂/J₁ = 0 the model maps to the exactly solvable XYZ chain via spin rotations.

### Emergent Quantized Zak Phases without Dimerization in a Multicomponent Fermionic Extended Hubbard Model

**Authors:** F. X. Liu, Z. Song
