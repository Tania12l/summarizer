### Spontaneous Time-Reversal-Symmetry-Breaking State in Proximity-Induced Superconductivity

**Authors:** Jonathan Schirmer, Wei Pan, Javad Shabani, Enrico Rossi

- Proximity to an \(s\)-wave superconductor can spontaneously break time‑reversal symmetry in multiband Dirac‑semimetal heterostructures without magnetic order.
- Derived conditions and phase diagrams show the TR‑breaking state as the ground state, including reentrant transitions between TR‑symmetric and TR‑breaking phases.
- Offers design rules for nonreciprocal superconducting transport in nonmagnetic heterostructures.

### Can the chemical potential and the many-body correlated phases of a two-dimensional dipolar fluid be approximated analytically? A Quantum Monte-Carlo study of a universal behavior of dipolar excitons

**Authors:** Yotam Mazuz-Harpaz, Michal Zimmerman, Snir Gazit, Ronen Rapaport

- QMC reveals a universal dipolar fluid behavior driven by a depletion zone that suppresses short‑range physics.
- Analytical interaction energies agree with QMC across classical, quantum gas, and liquid regimes; the plate‑capacitor model overestimates.
- Chemical potential is accurately described by a three‑term decomposition: kinetic, dipolar interaction, and high‑density confinement.

### Spin superfluid transport of magnons in altermagnetic insulators

**Authors:** Dele Li, Meng-Han Zhang, Dao-Xin Yao

- Spin superfluidity in Lieb/inverse‑Lieb altermagnets arises from easy‑plane Nèel order; the Goldstone mode becomes quadratic at the stability boundary.
- Axial phase gradients transfer spectral weight to a gapped mode, while diagonal flow does not; critical spin flow varies with injection angle due to crystal‑field effects.
- The decay length scales with interfacial spin‑mixing conductance (measurable via SHE/SSE), making La₂O₃FeMnSe₂ a promising low‑dissipation spin‑transport platform.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33294)

### Bosonic Magic Resources at Quantum Criticality

**Authors:** Paolo Stornati, Piotr Sierant, Emanuele Tirrito

- Uses quantum relative entropy of one‑body correlations to quantify non‑Gaussianity in the 1D extended Bose‑Hubbard ground state.
- QRE scaling detects phase transitions: Luttinger‑liquid superfluid, a subleading peak at the Mott‑Haldane transition (hidden string order), and Ising‑like scaling at the Haldane‑density‑wave transition.
- Establishes non‑Gaussianity as a probe of many‑body criticality, linking bosonic quantum resources to strongly correlated matter.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33306)

### Fractional Chern Insulator in a Finite-Range One-Band Model

**Authors:** Tong Liu, Hoi Chun Po

- Realizes a fractional Chern insulator in a single‑band, finite‑range interaction model on a square lattice without single‑particle quantum geometry.
- At 1/3 filling, exhibits a three‑fold ground‑state degeneracy, Hall conductance \(-2e^2/3h\), and fractional charge pumping (verified by ED and DMRG).
- Attributes the FCI phase to nontrivial topology arising in the repulsive two‑particle sector, offering a microscopic view of interaction‑driven fractional quantum Hall order.

**Authors:** Shadan Ghassemi Tabrizi

- Spin‑orbit coupling mixes orbitals of different symmetry, enabling ring‑opening of chalcogen trimers that are otherwise symmetry‑forbidden.
- In Se₃, Te₃, and Po₃ the coupling lowers barriers by 2–1 kcal mol⁻¹ and merges ground‑state and triplet surfaces.
- The resulting barrier reductions and triplet crossings explain reaction feasibility and influence predicted ozone lifetimes.
