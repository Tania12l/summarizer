### Ferromagnetism in Fe-doped SnO2 thin films

**Authors:** J. M. D. Coey, A. P. Douvalis, C. B. Fitzgerald, M. Venkatesan

- Fe‑doped SnO₂ thin films are transparent ferromagnets with TC ≈ 610 K and Ms ≈ 2 Am²/kg.
- Mossbauer spectra reveal high‑spin Fe³⁺; only ~23 % of Fe ions are magnetically ordered, each carrying ~8 µB.
- Ferromagnetism attributed to Fe³⁺ coupling through an electron trapped in an oxygen vacancy (F‑center).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401293v1)

### Ultrafast Conductivity Dynamics in Pentacene Probed using Terahertz Spectroscopy

**Authors:** V. K. Thorsmolle, R. D. Averitt, X. Chi, D. J. Hilton, D. L. Smith, A. P. Ramirez, A. J. Taylor

### Vortex waves and the onset of turbulence in $^3$He-B

**Authors:** K. L. Henderson, C. F. Barenghi

- Finne et al. identified the intrinsic turbulence onset criterion \(q=\alpha/(1-\alpha')<1\) in \(^{3}\)He‑B.
- The criterion corresponds to marginally damped Kelvin waves; for \(q>1\) waves cannot grow to trigger reconnections.
- Analysis of rotating‑superfluid modes confirms \(q=1\) as the crossover between damped and propagating Kelvin waves in the long‑wavelength limit.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401212v1)

### Contrasting one- and two-particle excitations in cuprate strange metals

**Authors:** Falk Röder. Roman Schuster, Martin Knupfer, Axel Lubk, Jörg Fink

- Optical plasmons in optimally doped cuprates show weak correlation‑induced dispersion but strong low‑energy damping governed by marginal Fermi liquid scattering.
- Acoustic plasmons exhibit linear‑in‑energy damping consistent with the same marginal Fermi liquid framework.
- One‑particle ARPES data confirm resilient quasiparticles, while two‑particle EELS/RIXS results are well described by combined electron–hole self‑energies.

### Revealing laser-induced modification of weak ferromagnetism through a spin-reorientation phase transition in iron borate Fe$_3$BO$_6$

**Authors:** A. V. Kuzikova, S. N. Barilo, R. A. Lanovsky, A. V. Sadovnikov, R. V. Pisarev, A. M. Kalashnikova

- Femtosecond laser pulses reduce the weak ferromagnetic moment in Fe₃BO₆ via 2p→3d charge‑transfer transitions, triggering a 90° spin‑reorientation (Γ₂→Γ₄).
- The transition shows a 35 % drop in weak ferromagnetism, with its threshold and latency governed by the diminished moment.
- The final state is a coexistence of Γ₄ and Γ₂ phases, reached through precessional switching driven by laser‑induced heating.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12460v1)

### Real Topology on Symmetry-Invariant Loops Enforces Superconducting Pairing Zeros

**Authors:** Hongyu Lu, Hao Jin, Wang Yao

- 1D real topology on antiunitary‑symmetry‑invariant loops enforces pairing zeros, independent of Fermi‑surface or global band topology.
- General conditions and lower bounds for pairing zeros are derived for any dimension, pairing channel, and Cooper‑pair momentum, without requiring isolated bands.
- Isolated zeros carry odd vorticity, yielding Bogoliubov‑de Gennes nodes and zero‑energy boundary modes, as shown in a two‑orbital model.

### Positronium resonance of electric monopoles in quantum spin ice

**Authors:** José Maria Cruz, Hidemaro Suwa, Masafumi Udagawa

- Electric monopoles in quantum spin ice exhibit a double‑peak spectrum: a lower‑energy bound state (positronium‑like) and a higher‑energy scattering continuum.
- Quantum Monte Carlo with stochastic analytic continuation at the Rokhsar–Kivelson point resolves the exciton‑like bound state of an electric monopole pair.
- The flippable loop operator locally excites monopole pairs, enabling detection via lattice or optical response.
