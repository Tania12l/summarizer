### Von Neumann's Two Quantum Entropies

**Authors:** Biao Wu

- Von Neumann introduced two quantum entropies (1927 and to define thermodynamic entropy.
- The 1927 entropy (von Neumann entropy) vanishes for pure states and is best viewed as an entanglement measure.
- The 1929 entropy, nonzero for almost all pure states, should be regarded as the true thermodynamic entropy.

### Disentangling the Toric Code

**Authors:** Lei Gioia, Salvatore D. Pace, Ruben Verresen, Shu-Heng Shao, Ryan Thorngren

- Finite‑dimensional ancillae cannot render commuting‑projector Hamiltonians (Ising, toric code) on‑site because fractionalized excitations obstruct it.
- Infinite‑dimensional ancillae (e.g., rotors) enable disentanglement, producing on‑site symmetries for both models.
- The findings align lattice and QFT anomaly analyses, while finite ancillae may lead to non‑invertible on‑site obstructions.

### A Unified Spin-Fermion Framework for Magnetic Diversity in Chromium Monopnictides

**Authors:** Ruoshi Jiang, Bartomeu Monserrat

- Chromium monopnictides (CrSb, CrAs, CrP) evolve from altermagnetism → double‑helical AFM → no long‑range order as ligand itinerancy increases with chemical pressure.
- A unified spin‑fermion model shows that Cr\(^{1+}\) \(d^5\) moments coupled to itinerant pnictogen states undergo exchange‑parameter reconstruction across NiAs → MnP structures.
- The resulting magnetic phase diagram explains the contrasting orders via competing Cr–Cr exchanges and proximity to a frustrated regime in CrP.
