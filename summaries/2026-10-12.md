### Flat-band quantum geometry from embedded quench dynamics

### Frustrated Topological Orbital Magnetism

**Authors:** Sergio Garrido Bourkhaeva, Claudio Castelnovo, Wojciech J. Jankowski

- Frustration + Aharonov‑Bohm fluxes produce topological semimetals with orbital dipole, quadrupole, and ferrotoroidic orders.
- Non‑Abelian nodal‑line toroidizations generate current‑induced Hall effects; 2‑in‑2‑out/3‑in‑1‑out patterns correspond to Weyl charges, yielding anisotropic linear/nonlinear Hall responses.
- Experimental signatures are proposed to identify these distinct frustrated topological orbital magnetic orders in candidate materials.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12557)

### Enhanced Coulomb pseudopotentials in high-pressure hydrides from first principles

**Authors:** Kieran Bozier, Chris J. Pickard

- First‑principles RPA calculations reveal Coulomb pseudopotentials μ* far above the empirical 1–15 range, e.g., μ*≈39 for Mg₂IrH₆.
- Strong Coulomb repulsion scales with the Fermi‑level density of states, suppressing Tc by up to ~40 K compared to standard μ* assumptions.
- These findings reconcile earlier Tc discrepancies and indicate that conventional μ* values overestimate Tc in high‑density‑of‑states hydrides.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12563)

### Improving the lower bound on the Laughlin gap: An SDP search

**Authors:** Qiang Gao

- SDP optimization of auxiliary operators boosts the proven Laughlin gap constant from 046 to ≈219, giving a gap ≥ 1/5 on large spheres.
- Product‑twirl and staircase bounds sharpen intermediate estimates, enabling the larger constant without altering the original certificate.
- Exact rational arithmetic certifies the result; heuristic and ED data suggest the true gap may approach ≈4, beyond the current auxiliary‑operator framework.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12700)
