### Lattice Symmetry Remnants in Spatial and Temporal Entanglement of Quantum Critical Spin Chains

**Authors:** Xiao-Han Yang, Rui-Zhen Huang

- Lattice symmetries imprint universal signatures on spatial and temporal entanglement spectra of critical spin chains, beyond bulk CFT data.
- Euclidean‑evolved states map to boundary CFTs; changing the short‑range initial state selects distinct conformal boundaries that are spectroscopically distinguishable.
- In the XXZ chain, the Lieb–Schultz–Mattis constraint forces a doubled unit cell in spatial entanglement, while the temporal state stays uniform, leading to pumping between translation‑related Dirichlet sectors.

### Optical signatures of Rashba--Dresselhaus coupling across Franz--Keldysh and Landau regimes in two-dimensional semiconductors

**Authors:** Bahruz Suleymanli, Samir Tariverdiev, B. Tanatar

- Unified theory of interband optical response in 2D semiconductors with simultaneous Rashba–Dresselhaus SOC under crossed electric and magnetic fields.
- Zero‑field subgap signatures: Rashba yields an inverse‑square‑root singularity; Dresselhaus adds a step and logarithmic peak, allowing optical discrimination of the two couplings.
- Field‑dependent behavior: Franz–Keldysh crossover from diabatic to adiabatic spin dynamics; Rashba splitting changes from √B at weak B to linear at strong B; Landau absorption lines shift, split, and anticross without altering total weight.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31843)

### Kinetic competition controls loss-induced exceptional points of Hubbard pairs

**Authors:** Ho-Kin Tang, Xiaosen Yang

- Two‑body loss can bind fermions in a Hubbard lattice, with a binding threshold \(g_c=1/[\pi\rho_2(]\).
- The threshold becomes an exceptional point when the kinetic energy along one lattice direction dominates the transverse directions, making the pair Green function purely imaginary at the band center.
- This kinetic competition allows total momentum to select EP sectors, restoring parity‑time symmetry of the scattering solutions and linking loss‑induced binding to exceptional degeneracy.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32123)
