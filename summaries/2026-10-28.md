### Same-spin correlation screens the exchange peak of the electron gas near 2kF

**Authors:** Carl A. Kukkonen

- Exchange alone yields a sharp local‑field peak just below 2k_F, absent in QMC.
- Dynamically screened lowest‑order diagrams show parallel‑spin correlation cancels ~½ of the peak, reproducing QMC at rs = 1, 2 and q = 0 compressibility/spin susceptibility.
- The omitted second term of Hedin’s vertex kernel is essential for accurate spin‑resolved local‑field factors.

### An exactly solvable obstructed seam on a lattice Klein bottle: bulk-locked Majorana rings and register-dependent toric codes

**Authors:** Junhao (Eigen), Wang

- Exactly solvable frustrated seam on a lattice Klein bottle reduces to free Majorana rings with bulk‑locked sectors.
- Ground‑state energies, gaps, and degeneracies are analytically obtained and confirmed by finite‑size diagonalization; seam degeneracy is non‑topological and split by single‑site fields.
- Register‑dependent toric‑code realizations and logical‑gate analysis show distinct ground‑state degeneracies and holonomy doublets on 16‑ and 24‑qubit lattices.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35931)

### Planckian dissipation per unit critical entropy as a coupling-free probe of the strange-metal bath

**Authors:** Dino \v{S}krgi\'c, Dejan Stojkovic

- Strange metals exhibit linear‑T resistivity and logarithmic‑T specific heat; the ratio \(Q\) of their coefficients is coupling‑independent.
- \(Q\) is set solely by the bath’s relaxation rate and the fraction of critical entropy that relaxes current, with distinct ranges for conformal (\(\le91\)), Yukawa–SYK (9–, and Kondo baths.
- LSCO’s measured \(Q\approx9\) exceeds the conformal bound, pointing to a Yukawa–SYK critical bath.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35939)

### Bayesian Inference in Partially Observed Critical Systems

**Authors:** Mihika Singhania

- Bayesian estimator of average displacement in random transposition shuffle has relative uncertainty \(1/\sqrt{2m}\), independent of deck size \(n\).
- A variance floor \(R_n=(2n^2+/(5(n-^2(n+)\) limits precision; the posterior remains calibrated.
- In the 2‑D Ising critical point, energy floor vanishes while magnetization floor ≈072 (mean‑field limit \(\sqrt2-1\)); sensor‑network intervals cover the ensemble mean only 7 % of the time.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35973)

### Amorphization-Depth-Dependent Bismuth Redistribution and Activation in Silicon for Quantum Devices

**Authors:** Youcef A. Bioud, Simon-Jean Michel, Sebastien Francoeur, Sjoerd Roorda, Eva Dupont-Ferrier

- 650 °C anneal yields ~90 % substitutional Bi, yet electrical activation is 60 % for shallow (47 nm) vs 90 % for deep (177 nm) preamorphization.
- A buried Bi feature at the amorphous/crystalline interface indicates defect trapping; surface segregation rises with higher annealing temperatures.
- XPS/PL show no Bi‑oxide and reveal donor‑related optical signatures, highlighting that regrowth geometry, not just substitutionality, controls Bi activation.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35993)

### Mesons, Confinement, and Complexity: A Krylov Space Perspective on the Long-Range Ising Chain

**Authors:** Xuhao Jiang, Jad C. Halimeh, N. S. Srivatsa

- Krylov complexity is strongly suppressed and size‑independent in the strongly confined regime (small α, small transverse field).
- Oscillation frequencies of the complexity match meson masses; the dominant frequency scales linearly with the lightest meson mass, while the time‑averaged complexity scales as its inverse square.
- At finite temperature, operator Krylov complexity displays bounded oscillations that encode the meson spectrum at low T, which fade as temperature rises.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36054)

### A cascade of orbital-field-driven phases with a Lifshitz-type transition to unidirectional spiral order

**Authors:** Hiroki Nakai, Hae-Young Kee

- Orbital‑field coupling on the honeycomb Heisenberg model creates a subextensive degeneracy, lifted by order‑by‑disorder into a cascade of collinear magnetic phases.
- As the field increases, a Lifshitz‑type transition occurs to an incommensurate unidirectional spiral that breaks chiral symmetry, with spin stiffness becoming negative along one direction.
- The transition is driven by coupling of a Goldstone mode to gapped spin‑orbital fluctuations, yielding a spiral wavevector that grows as the square root of the distance from criticality.

### Towards Quantum Computation of Bulk FeO

**Authors:** Daniel Staros, Federico Petrovich, Ammar Kirmani, Daniel N. Blaschke, M. Cerezo, Jian-Xin Zhu

- SQD with two connectivity shells exactly reproduces FeO and Fe₂O₂ ground‑state energies using few qubits.
- Classical diagonalization and VQE recover FeO’s quintet ground state; VQE lacks chemical accuracy.
- Suggests ab initio‑informed low‑energy models + SQD as a scalable method for correlated solids on near‑term hardware.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36268)
