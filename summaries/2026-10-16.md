### All-temperature superfluidity in two dimensions

**Authors:** Bilal Hawashin, Soumyadeep Chaudhuri, Igor F. Herbut, Michael M. Scherer

- BKT superfluidity survives to arbitrarily high temperatures in a (2+‑D theory where a complex scalar couples to a critical O(N) model.
- At the biconical fixed point the phase stiffness scales linearly with temperature and with N, rendering vortex operators irrelevant for large N.
- Vortex fugacity is exponentially suppressed in N, ensuring stability of the high‑temperature superfluid phase.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20717)

### Boundary-induced geometrical frustration in a square Rydberg-atom lattice

**Authors:** Marco Rigobello, Luka Pave\v{s}i\'{c}, Daniel Jaschke, Pietro Silvi, Marco Di Liberto, Simone Montangero, Simone Notarnicola

- Odd‑size lattices select a single striated domain; even‑size lattices seed incompatible corner domains, yielding a π/2‑rotation invariant state with a central defect.

### Long-lived local coherences at high temperature from frozen domain walls

**Authors:** Sarang Gopalakrishnan, Romain Vasseur

- Shows that local coherences in spin chains with quantum‑group symmetry decay only algebraically, not faster than any power law.
- Derives a Mazur bound $C(t)\gtrsim1/t^2$ and computes explicit decay $C(t)\sim1/\sqrt{t}$ (large anisotropy) and $C(t)\sim t^{-2/z}$ (general anisotropy).
- Demonstrates that frozen domain walls in integrable and chaotic models (XXZ, Fredkin) protect long‑lived coherences even at high temperature.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20724)

### Resonant enhancement of Raman scattering in a MoS$_2$ nanotube

**Authors:** D. R. Kazanov, A. V. Poshakinskiy

- Whispering‑gallery modes in a MoS₂ nanotube wall doubly resonantly enhance Raman scattering, scaling as \((Q_{\rm pump}Q_{\rm sc})^2\).
- Excitonic dispersion near the A exciton compresses the mode ladder, allowing a phonon‑energy match (A₁g at 1551 meV) without geometric tuning.
- A detuned resonance splits the peak into a pump‑ and scattered‑resonant doublet, confined to the TM→TM channel and excluding E₁g.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20755)

### Plasmon Collapse in a Directional Strange Metal

**Authors:** Jiade Li, Peiyi He, Deng Hu, Jiakai Wang, Jifeng Shao, Wan-Shun Du, Xuetao Zhu, Jiandong Guo, Zhiwei Wang, Peng Gao

- MoOCl₂ exhibits anisotropic transport: coherent Fermi‑liquid behavior along one in‑plane axis and incoherent strange‑metal behavior along the orthogonal axis.
- In the strange‑metal direction the plasmon becomes overdamped, reverses dispersion at \(q_c\approx008\,\text{Å}^{-1}\), and collapses into a broad continuum with a high‑energy cutoff \(E_c\approx75\,\text{eV}\).
- Both \(q_c\) and \(E_c\) vary with sample thickness, indicating dimensional control over the plasmon collapse.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21209)

### Thermodynamic-limit bootstrap: Solving fractional quantum Hall ground states

**Authors:** Qiang Gao, Taige Wang

- Quantum‑many‑body bootstrap solves fractional quantum Hall ground states directly in the thermodynamic limit, giving rigorous energy lower bounds and identifying topological order.
- Benchmarks on Laughlin, Pfaffian, and Coulomb systems reproduce exact pair correlations, orbital‑entanglement spectra, and match DMRG/ED extrapolations.
- Applied to ν=1/4 and ν=12/5, it uncovers a composite Fermi liquid and \(\overline{\mathrm{RR}}_3\) topological order, showing versatility for both gapped and gapless states.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21317)

### Flat-band ferrimagnetism in the edge-centered honeycomb antiferromagnet: From harmonic flat magnon to broad continuum

**Authors:** Sicheng He, Zhixuan Zhao, Hanxiang Di, Shuaihua Zhang, Yanling Wu, Jun Li

- Edge‑centered honeycomb spin‑½ Heisenberg AFM has a 2:3 sublattice imbalance that yields a flat magnon band in linear spin‑wave theory.
- Lieb–Mattis theorem guarantees a ferrimagnetic ground state, with the flat mode localized on edge sites.
- Quantum Monte Carlo and analytic continuation reveal the harmonic flat mode dissolves into a broad continuum, showing interaction‑driven breakdown of the flat band.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21580)

### Interlocked Vortex Order in the Frustrated $A_3$V$_9$Te$_{13}$ Lattice ($A$=Cs,Rb)

**Authors:** Zilong Li, H. -Q. Yuan, Xiaoqun Wang, Chao Cao

- Frustrated V sublattice favors a vortex/anti‑vortex antiferromagnetic ground state, lowest in energy.
- This phase hosts kagome‑like electronic features: Dirac crossings, van Hove singularities, flat bands; Cs‑based is metallic with 6 Weyl points, Rb‑based shows a ~27 meV SOC gap.
- The spin pattern is rigid—fixing one triangle fixes all—and the Néel temperature is nearly field‑independent, matching experiments.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21854)

### Putting a Temperature on Electron Correlation

**Authors:** Paul G. Graf, Florian Matz, Markus Penz, Christian Schilling

- Links electron‑correlation measures to a conjugate “correlation temperature” in reduced‑density‑matrix functional theory, enabling mean‑field‑level treatments.
- Expresses correlation energy as a function of the one‑particle reduced density matrix, rationalizing methods such as i‑DMFT.
- Validates the framework analytically on the Hubbard dimer and numerically on small molecules, assessing how much correlation can be captured at mean‑field cost.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22134)
