### Ferromagnetism in Fe-doped SnO2 thin films

**Authors:** J. M. D. Coey, A. P. Douvalis, C. B. Fitzgerald, M. Venkatesan

- Fe‑doped SnO₂ thin films are transparent ferromagnets with TC ≈ 610 K and Ms ≈ 2 Am²/kg.
- Mossbauer spectra reveal high‑spin Fe³⁺; only ~23 % of Fe ions are magnetically ordered, each carrying ~8 µB.
- Ferromagnetism attributed to Fe³⁺ coupling through an electron trapped in an oxygen vacancy (F‑center).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401293v1)

### Vortex waves and the onset of turbulence in $^3$He-B

**Authors:** K. L. Henderson, C. F. Barenghi

- Finne et al. identified the intrinsic turbulence onset criterion \(q=\alpha/(1-\alpha')<1\) in \(^{3}\)He‑B.
- The criterion corresponds to marginally damped Kelvin waves; for \(q>1\) waves cannot grow to trigger reconnections.
- Analysis of rotating‑superfluid modes confirms \(q=1\) as the crossover between damped and propagating Kelvin waves in the long‑wavelength limit.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401212v1)

### Upper critical dimension for dirty Weyl semimetal-to-metal quantum phase transitions

**Authors:** Yongtai Li, Bitan Roy

- Lower and upper critical dimensions for the disorder‑driven Weyl semimetal‑to‑metal transition are \(d_{\ell}=2\) and \(d_{u}=4\).
- Numerical ADOS yields a correlation‑length exponent \(\nu\approx5\) for \(d\ge4\) and a dynamic exponent \(z\approx d/2\) for \(d\ge3\).
- Field‑theoretic RG confirms \(z=d/2\) exactly, enabling analytical treatment near both critical dimensions.

### Disorder-induced quantum Fisher information in topological quantum systems

**Authors:** Advay Burte, Keshav Das Agarwal, Leela Ganesh Chandra Lakkaraju, Aditi Sen De

- Disorder leaves the toric‑code ground state unchanged but boosts the QFI for estimating perturbations when a separable probe evolves under the disordered Hamiltonian.

- The disorder‑enhanced QFI remains high over a range of perturbation strengths, encoding times, and survives dephasing, bit‑flip, and amplitude‑damping noise.

### Characterizing quasiparticles in strongly correlated systems using nonlinear spectroscopy in quantum simulators

**Authors:** Luka Skolc, Utso Bhattacharya, Jonathan B. Curtis, Immanuel Bloch, Eugene Demler

- Nonlinear spectroscopy and fluctuation protocols identify quasiparticle charge sign without synthetic gauge fields.
- Second‑order density/current responses and third‑order cumulants distinguish electron‑ vs hole‑like carriers; hole‑doping in a Fermi‑Hubbard ladder reverses the sign, matching Hall coefficient behavior.
- The method applies to hard‑core bosons, where finite‑momentum, finite‑frequency drives reveal carrier sign and are amplified near resonant collective modes.

### Orientational Ordering of Janus Particle Arrays as Classical Compass Spin Systems

**Authors:** Zhijie Fan, Myeonggon Park, Marija Vucelja, Gia-Wei Chern

- Janus particle orientations map to classical compass spins with bond‑dependent interactions, modeled by a 120° compass and Kern–Frenkel potentials.
- Monte Carlo simulations reveal a low‑temperature sixfold ordered phase, a high‑temperature disordered phase, and an intermediate quasi‑long‑range regime with two BKT transitions driven by thermal order‑by‑disorder.
- Adding higher‑order terms alters the emergent anisotropy: a quadratic term reverses the sixfold selection, while a cubic term lifts the degeneracy and selects lattice‑aligned directions observed experimentally.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30025v1)

### Exact phase diagram of the XXZ Heisenberg chain in a staggered magnetic field

**Authors:** H. P. Zhang, Z. Song

- Exact analytical relation between exchange anisotropy and staggered field yields a highly degenerate ground‑state manifold with off‑diagonal long‑range order on the phase boundary.
- The boundary separates a ferromagnetic phase from a gapless Luttinger liquid, with a first‑order transition confirmed by DMRG.
- Reducing anisotropy further drives the gapless phase into a gapped quantum‑disordered phase, completing the full phase diagram.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29728v1)
