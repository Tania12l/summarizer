### Spectral Crossings Diagnose Multiply Quantized Vortex Splitting

**Authors:** Jongheon Baek, Hiroyuki Tajima, Gihwan Nam

- Spectral zero‑energy crossings in a two‑component Haldane sphere reveal whether a charge‑\(r\) vortex is fused or split into \(r\) unit zeros.
- The critical detuning scales as \(|\tau_c|\sim N^{-(r+}\) for a fused vortex and \(\sim N^{-2}\) for resolved unit zeros, confirmed by Feshbach reduction and numerics for \(r=1,2,3\).
- When a partially split cluster’s diameter \(d\) reaches the magnetic length \(\ell_B\), the scaling crosses over, enabling spectroscopic inference of \(d\) below imaging resolution.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02234)

### Reply to "Comment on 'Topography of Fermi arcs in t-PtBi2 using high-resolution angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy'"

**Authors:** Adam Kaminski, Paul C. Canfield, Benjamin Schrunk

- Confirmed Fermi arcs in t‑PtBi₂ are disconnected segments crossing EF only between Weyl points, with a finite band gap elsewhere.
- Found no superconducting gap down to 3 K; the Comment’s claim stems from misidentifying arc locations and using unsuitable interpolated data.
- Results agree with STM showing surface superconductivity only below 3 K, the temperature at which ARPES data were taken.

### Counterdiabatic Quantum Circuits

**Authors:** Ankit Wenju Shrestha, Budhaditya Bhattacharjee, Adolfo del Campo

- Presents a Hamiltonian‑free theory for adiabatic gauge potentials (AGP) of parameter‑dependent unitary operators.
- Computes exact/regularized AGP via a Hermitian banded linear system, avoiding diagonalization or matrix logarithms, with rapid Krylov convergence.
- Extends counterdiabatic shortcuts and AGP diagnostics to Floquet systems and quantum circuits, demonstrated on the kicked top and integrable brickwork circuits.

### Topological phase transition in a symmetric blockade structure

**Authors:** Johannes M\"ogerle, Simon Fell, Tobias F. Maier, Ali Otaifi, Lode Pollet, Nicolai Lang, Hans Peter B\"uchler

- iDMRG on a cylinder maps the full phase diagram of a blockade Hamiltonian with local ℤ₂ symmetry.
- A single continuous transition separates a topologically ordered phase from a trivial paramagnet, stable up to Rabi drives ≈ the system’s energy scale.
- The charge gap remains open across the transition, while the symmetric sector gap closes, signaling flux condensation and charge confinement in the trivial phase.
