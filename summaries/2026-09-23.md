### Scaling of qubit coherence in quantum dot based Majorana chains: Rabi and Ramsey oscillations of Majorana qubits formed by four and six quantum dots

**Authors:** Haining Pan, Sankar Das Sarma

- Rabi oscillations remain length‑independent in the pristine limit, but disorder limits coherence via three competing dephasing channels.
- Ramsey oscillations in six‑dot chains suffer larger leakage because the third site suppresses unwanted splittings yet requires larger detuning that excites bulk states.
- Neither Rabi nor Ramsey coherence universally improves with chain length; extra dots help only if inter‑chain tunneling fluctuations are suppressed, while Ramsey coherence depends on detuning‑induced energy‑splitting fluctuations.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25223)
