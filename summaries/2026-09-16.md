**Authors:** Reggie C. Pantig, Ali \"Ovg\"un

- Extends relativistic nonaffine shear‑viscosity theory to a Maxwell‑Jüttner averaged framework for massive classical gases.
- Replaces a single Lorentz factor by its equilibrium momentum average, producing a finite‑mass enhancement function of ζ = mc²/(k_BT).
- Derives nonrelativistic/ultrarelativistic limits, validates hard‑sphere scaling, and shows the memory kernel is distinct from a simple relaxation time.

### Anderson localization in topological metals

**Authors:** A. A. Burkov

- 3D Weyl semimetals preserve long‑range entanglement and resist Anderson localization under disorder.
- 2D/3D Dirac and 3D nodal‑line semimetals do not retain this protection and become localized.
- The conclusion is derived from unquantized anomaly responses and decorated domain‑wall arguments.

### Optical and magnetic signatures of drive-enhanced coherence in phase-disordered superconducting bilayers

**Authors:** Duilio De Santis, Sambuddha Chattopadhyay, Marios H. Michael, Andrea Cavalleri, Gil Refael, Patrick A. Lee, Eugene A. Demler

- Drive‐induced stiffness oscillations in bilayers boost low‑frequency \(1/\omega\) optical conductivity.

### Quantum Critical Solids

**Authors:** Subham Dutta Chowdhury, Leo Radzihovsky, Luca V. Delacr\'etaz

- Floppy quantum solids with zero transverse phonon velocity acquire a relevant cubic elastic nonlinearity for \(d<4\), driving a strongly‑coupled Lifshitz quantum critical point in \(d=3\).
- The transition is generically first‑order, yet a Ginzburg region near the transition exposes the critical exponents of the Lifshitz point.
- In \(d=2\) the cubic term vanishes; quartic interactions become marginal, producing only weak logarithmic singularities.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16147)

### Breaking the Wiedemann-Franz limit in thermoelectrics via separated flat bands

**Authors:** Illia Serhiienko, Fabian Garmroudi, Stefan Enzner, Izem Dural, Giorgio Sangiovanni, Andrej Pustogow

- Uses energy‑dependent scattering in flat‑band materials (e.g., monolayer Ni₃In) to decouple electronic thermal and electrical conductivities, violating the Wiedemann‑Franz law.
- Achieves a boxcar‑shaped transport distribution near the Fermi level, yielding high Seebeck coefficient and conductivity while suppressing electronic heat conduction.
- Analyzes ~50 000 data sets in a zT vs. S² plot to identify anomalously low Lorenz numbers, highlighting new physics or measurement artifacts.

### Hartree-Fock Density-Matrix Renormalization Group for Very Long Chains

**Authors:** Steven R. White

- Reformulates Hartree–Fock as a DMRG sweep of a single Slater determinant with density–density interactions.
- Compresses occupied states and long‑range Coulomb terms separately, reducing each sweep to a small local Fock problem while retaining exchange.
- Enables converging 100 000‑electron hydrogen chains (8 M basis functions) in ~2 h on a 64‑GB Mac mini, with work per sweep scaling linearly with chain length.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16587)

### Single-Instance Observables in the Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev Model

**Authors:** Brian Swingle

- Diagrammatic expansion for thermal expectation values of low‑weight Majorana strings in a fixed SYK realization, organized in powers of \(N^{-1/2}\) and evaluable with classical resources polynomial in \(N\).
- Extension to arbitrary imaginary‑time and short real‑time correlations, with excellent agreement to exact diagonalization up to \(N=24\) in the four‑body SYK model.
- Application to the Maldacena–Qi model reveals a nearby SYK‑like Hamiltonian whose thermofield double state has higher fidelity with the MQ ground state than the original SYK thermofield double.

### Exact group invariant scar towers in two dimensional gauge theories

**Authors:** Jo\~{a}o Barata, Kiryl Pakrouski, Andrey V. Sadofyev

- Constructed exact scar towers in 2D gauge theories with two flavors of massless fermions via a gauge‑neutral η‑pairing operator.
- Scar states exhibit low entanglement, long‑range pair correlations, and map under charge conjugation to a coherent flavor mode with Josephson‑like dynamics.
- Finite fermion masses mix the protected pair and destroy the tower, but the construction applies universally across many lattice field theories.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16132)

### Designs without disorder: unitary $k$-designs from a single-site bulk defect

**Authors:** Samudra Sur, Pratik Nandy

- A single O(bulk defect in an integrable XXZ chain produces approximate unitary k‑designs via a two‑Pauli‑kick protocol with temporal sampling.
- Design formation is governed by symmetry‑preserving Pauli strings with a specific domain‑wall count; exhaustive search finds many such kicks in the chaotic regime but none in the integrable limit.
- The mechanism remains effective for weak bulk defects as system size increases, whereas boundary defects do not generate designs.
