### Thermal Diffusivity in Metallic Nanowire Aerogels

**Authors:** Madeline A. Maben, WLNC Namila Liyanage, Cameron S. Jorgensen, Erin Marlowe, Kai Liu, Gerd Duscher, Dustin A. Gilbert

- Ag/Cu nanowire aerogels (20–500 mg cm⁻³) exhibit lower thermal diffusivity for 30 nm wires than 60 nm, due to surface scattering.
- Compaction densifies the aerogel yet reduces diffusivity ~300×, driven mainly by the thickness term rather than transport coefficient changes.
- Densification preserves the percolative network, showing aerogels cannot be treated as homogeneous bulk materials.

### Exact solutions for kinematics across interaction-deformed Fermi surfaces

**Authors:** Alexander Kruchkov, F. Duncan M. Haldane

- Exact rates of change for occupation deficit inside and surplus outside the interacting Fermi surface are derived when interactions are tuned at fixed density.
- These rates equal the weight swept by the deforming surface plus the change of Luttinger’s integrand at fixed momentum; the surface term difference equals the flux of incoherent weight \(1-Z_{\vec k}\).
- The resulting identities hold with or without Luttinger’s theorem, offering precise kinematic relations for interaction‑deformed Fermi surfaces.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08921)

### Phase diagram of a semiflexible magnetic polymer in three dimensions

**Authors:** Linda Delimi, Arpan Dey, Jean-Charles Walter

- Coupled conformational, stiffness, and two‑state spin degrees of freedom in a semiflexible magnetic polymer on an FCC lattice.
- Zero‑field phase diagram: first‑order coil‑to‑globule/ferromagnetic collapse at low stiffness, globule‑to‑crystal transition at lower T, and direct coil‑to‑crystal transition at high stiffness.
- Monte Carlo and variational free‑energy analysis shows how bending rigidity and magnetic interactions reorganize polymer phases, linking flexible magnetic polymers to semiflexible homopolymers.

### Ambipolar metal-insulator transition in bilayer MoSe2

**Authors:** Ha-Leem Kim, Hyungbin Lim, Terry Wu, Amogh Y. Waghmare, Garima Gupta, Takashi Taniguchi, Kenji Watanabe, Archana Raja, Eric Y. Ma, Feng Wang

- Contact‑free RF reflectometry measures sheet resistance in bilayer MoSe₂, revealing both electron and hole metal‑insulator transitions.
- Critical densities differ fourfold yet share similar Coulomb‑to‑kinetic energy ratios, matching solid‑liquid coexistence seen by STM.
- Magnetoresistance peaks at each transition, indicating a field‑driven Pomeranchuk‑like expansion of the Wigner solid against the correlated liquid.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09036)
