### Hydrodynamic memory in overdamped colloidal dynamics

**Authors:** Benjamin Sorkin, G\"unther Turk, Howard A. Stone

- Hydrodynamic memory survives in overdamped colloidal dynamics, yielding power‑law decay of velocity autocorrelation.
- A closed‑form overdamped Langevin equation is derived, governed by a single dimensionless crossover parameter.
- The model predicts stronger memory for smaller particles and higher forcing, guiding experiments to detect the exponential‑to‑power‑law transition.

### What does "instant thermalization" in large-$q$ SYK models mean?

**Authors:** Alexander Osterkorn, Jan C. Louw

- Instantaneous thermalization occurs only for the block \(t_1,t_2>0\); the causal Green’s function becomes thermal immediately there.
- After a quench the effective temperature relaxes with a Planckian rate \(\Gamma\sim T/q\), not the expected divergent \(\Gamma\sim Tq\).
- Off‑diagonal blocks \(t_1t_2<0\) stay non‑thermal, setting a finite overall thermalization rate.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17794)
