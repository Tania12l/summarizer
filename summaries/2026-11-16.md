### Ferromagnetism in Fe-doped SnO2 thin films

**Authors:** J. M. D. Coey, A. P. Douvalis, C. B. Fitzgerald, M. Venkatesan

- Fe‑doped SnO₂ thin films are transparent ferromagnets with TC ≈ 610 K and Ms ≈ 2 Am²/kg.
- Mossbauer spectra reveal high‑spin Fe³⁺; only ~23 % of Fe ions are magnetically ordered, each carrying ~8 µB.
- Ferromagnetism attributed to Fe³⁺ coupling through an electron trapped in an oxygen vacancy (F‑center).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401293v1)

### Observation of narrow fluorescence from doubly driven four-level atoms at room temperature

**Authors:** Uday Kumar Khan, Jimmy Sebastian, N. Kamaraju, Andal Narayanan, R. Srinivasan, Hema Ramachandran

### Vortex waves and the onset of turbulence in $^3$He-B

**Authors:** K. L. Henderson, C. F. Barenghi

- Finne et al. identified the intrinsic turbulence onset criterion \(q=\alpha/(1-\alpha')<1\) in \(^{3}\)He‑B.
- The criterion corresponds to marginally damped Kelvin waves; for \(q>1\) waves cannot grow to trigger reconnections.
- Analysis of rotating‑superfluid modes confirms \(q=1\) as the crossover between damped and propagating Kelvin waves in the long‑wavelength limit.

### Classical weak antilocalization in electron hydrodynamics

**Authors:** Alberto Cortijo

- Classical memory kernel from spatial fluctuations of momentum‑relaxation rate gives a logarithmic, antilocalizing correction to hydrodynamic resistivity.
- In a Fermi liquid the correction scales as \(-T^2\ln(T_\xi/T)\); in a Planckian metal as \(-T\ln(T_\xi/T)\), cut off by momentum relaxation and viscosity.
- In narrow channels the return effect competes with viscosity renormalization, enabling separation through width scans.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17927)

### Thermally Hysteretic Room-Temperature Nonlinear Hall Effect in Bulk EuTe4

**Authors:** Yang Fu, Xingyu Lv, Shangjie Tian, Ying Ma, Xiao Zhang, Cedomir Petrovic, Hechang Lei

- Bulk EuTe₄ crystals display a giant nonlinear Hall effect up to 400 K.
- The NLHE shows strong thermal hysteresis, with a voltage ratio ≈ 7 between warming and cooling at 340 K.
- The behavior is attributed to an inversion‑symmetry‑broken charge density wave, positioning EuTe₄ as a room‑temperature nonlinear electronic platform.

### Edge spin generalization for quantum Hall Laughlin droplets with elliptic shapes

**Authors:** Alexander Fagerlund, Alberto Nardin, Leonardo Mazza, Eddy Ardonne

- Edge spin in quantum Hall Laughlin droplets can fractionalize, inducing an opposite spin at the boundary.
- Two generalizations are tested for elliptic droplets: one from single‑particle orbitals (valid for non‑interacting states) and one from emergent fluid properties (valid for interacting states).
- The fluid‑based definition successfully defines edge spin for Laughlin fluids with non‑symmetric, elliptic shapes.
