### Efficient spectral Galerkin framework for nonlinear transient heat transfer in finite domains

**Authors:** Th\'eo Andrieux, Andreas Ntinos, Manas V. Upadhyay

- Spectral Galerkin reformulates nonlinear heat transfer into a linear reference problem, yielding decoupled modal ODEs solved by exponential time differencing.
- Matches finite‑element accuracy (≤ 1 % error) while delivering 227× faster GPU runtimes for laser‑metal heating.
- Removes global FE solves and exploits structured grids for efficient GPU parallelization, enabling rapid large‑scale transient analyses.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22268)

### Predicting energy and structural response to force correction in molecular dynamics

**Authors:** Peng Kang, Da Wan, Shulin Bai, Pengfei Zhang, Peng Wang, Chenglong Wen, Zhen Li, Yu Liu, Lei Zheng, Li-Dong Zhao

- Uses leading response coefficients on shared reference trajectories to predict how force corrections alter energy exchange and structural statistics.
- Introduces residual power and displacement virial to separate energy transfer from restoring‑force changes, revealing timing‑dependent heating and missed structural shifts in anharmonic systems.
- Validated on silicon, SnSe, and anharmonic chains, showing trade‑offs between scalar calibration and directional accuracy and guiding optimal reference integration in MD.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22287)

### Universal Response Functions in Driven Dissipative Tunneling Dynamics

**Authors:** Krishna Kingkar Pathak

- Universal two‑parameter response function governs barrier‑crossing in periodically driven, dissipative tunneling.
- Semiclassical instanton with Floquet modulation and Ohmic coupling factorizes the tunneling exponent into a static term plus a universal function of driving frequency and dissipation strength.
- Numerical instanton action confirms dynamical averaging at high frequency and a continuous adiabatic‑to‑Floquet crossover.

### A rank-two spherical transform for eigenvector and singular-vector overlaps

**Authors:** Pierre Bousseyroux

- Introduces a rank‑two spherical transform \( \mathcal{H}^{(} \) and its dual \( \mathcal{R}^{(} \).
- \( \mathcal{R}^{(} \) yields a \(4\times4\) formula for the average product of two hermitized resolvents, giving off‑diagonal eigenvector overlaps for Ginibre, elliptic, and bi‑invariant ensembles.
- Extends the framework to correlated matrix pairs, providing overlaps between their eigenvectors or singular vectors.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22334)

### Existence and Uniqueness of Nearest Stealthy Hyperuniform Configurations from Random Initial Conditions

**Authors:** Fausto Martelli

- Proved that almost every random 2‑D periodic particle set has a unique nearest stealthy hyperuniform configuration (up to floppy modes) when a finite set of low‑k Fourier modes are constrained to zero.
- Established uniqueness of the orthogonal displacement under a full‑rank Jacobian, with remaining tangent‑space degrees as first‑order floppy modes preserving the constrained density fluctuations.
- Implemented a gradient‑based generator that iteratively drives random configurations toward disordered hyperuniform states, suppressing long‑wavelength density fluctuations beyond the explicitly constrained modes.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22365)

### Bootstrap certification of string order in quantum spin chains

**Authors:** Sagnik Banerjee, Haoyu Guo, Debanjan Chowdhury

- Polynomial‑time bootstrap treats string operators as primary variables, yielding rigorous bounds on long‑range string correlators.
- Certifies nonlocal string order in cluster Ising and spin‑1 Heisenberg chains, matching DMRG without a variational wavefunction.
- Bound sharpness depends on operator content, not string length, enabling wavefunction‑free certification of topological order.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22420)

### Hund-driven local-itinerant duality of Eu-4$f$ electrons in infinite-layer nickelates

**Authors:** Yingying Cao, Yi-feng Yang

- Hund’s coupling drives Eu‑4f electrons through three regimes: nonmagnetic, mixed‑valence with local moments, and fully spin‑polarized divalent.
- Mixed‑valence state originates from intra‑4f charge transfer between j=5/2 and j=7/2, creating a hole‑doped orbital‑selective Mott state.
- Low‑energy 4f quasiparticles coexist with Ni‑3d electrons, potentially enhancing superconductivity in Eu‑substituted infinite‑layer nickelates.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23645)

- MgMn₆Sn₆ is a kagome ferromagnet (TC ≈ 295 K) with easy‑plane anisotropy and strongly anisotropic MR that changes from negative (in‑plane) to quadratic (out‑of‑plane).
