### Magnetic localization and spectral asymmetry of a charged particle at a screw dislocation

**Authors:** Claudio Furtado, Omar Mustafa, Hassan Hassanabadi, Abdullah Guvendi

- Radial dynamics combine an inverse‑square term from the screw‑modified conserved quantity \(\mathcal{J}=\ell-\beta k\) with a magnetic harmonic confinement.
- For \(q\mathcal{B}_0\neq0\) the spectrum is exactly solvable: equally spaced levels with an orientation‑dependent shift; opposite \(\mathcal{J}\) values share spatial profiles but different energies.

### Axiomatic Incompatibility Between Thermal Equilibrium and Volume-Dependent Entropy in Ideal Gas Theory

**Authors:** Andrea Paglietti

- Volume‑dependent entropy for an ideal gas contradicts the thermal equilibrium axiom in reversible isothermal processes.
- The paper shows entropy must depend only on temperature, introducing a “pseudo‑isothermal” process (small adiabatic + isochoric steps) to recover classical work and heat relations.
- The corrected entropy matches Maxwell’s kinetic theory but differs from the Planck–Boltzmann microstate count, indicating a fundamental split between thermodynamic and statistical entropies.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10645)

### Efficiency fluctuations in quantum heat engines with non-quasi-static adiabatic strokes

**Authors:** Bikram Keshari Behera, Shyamal Biswas

- Analyzed efficiency fluctuations in quantum Carnot and Otto engines with pistons executing non‑quasi‑static adiabatic strokes at constant speed, alongside quasi‑static isothermal/isochoric steps.
- Modeled pistons as single quantum particles in a 1‑D box; two‑point measurements capture stochastic work from unitary evolution during non‑equilibrium strokes.
- Demonstrated that piston speed introduces additional quantum fluctuations, modifying the average efficiency of both engine types.

### SC$^\sharp$: superconductivity intertwined with topological order

**Authors:** Zhi-Qiang Gao, Yan-Qi Wang, Hui Yang, Zhaoyu Han, Ashvin Vishwanath

- Introduces SC$^\sharp$ as a class of (2+D gapped superconductors whose elementary vortices are intrinsically non‑Abelian and cannot be factorized into invertible superconductor vortices plus bulk anyons.
- Proves no‑go results: elementary vortex quantum dimension ≥ √6, ruling out Fibonacci fusion, and constructs infinite families via systematic anyon condensation.
- Defines primitive SC$^\sharp$ phases that cannot arise by disordering lower‑charge condensates, revealing new intertwined topological order.

### Symmetric Mode Ansatz: Theory of Magnon Collective Modes in Chern Insulators

**Authors:** Miguel Gon\c{c}alves, Jonah Herzog-Arbeitman, Juan Felipe Mendez-Valderrama, Andreas Feuerpfeil, B. Andrei Bernevig, Nicolas Regnault

- Introduces the symmetric mode ansatz (SyMA) to analytically describe magnon spectra in Chern insulators, matching numerical results at integer and fractional fillings.
- Proves SyMA exactness in specific Hubbard models (Haldane, ideal continuum) and derives magnon‑gap bounds from Chern number, Berry curvature, and quantum metric.
- Shows uniform quantum geometry stabilizes Ising ferromagnetism and gapped magnons in moiré Chern insulators such as twisted MoTe₂.

### Cooper Instability of a Magnetic Wigner Crystal

**Authors:** Wangqian Miao, Chunli Huang

- Explored Cooper pairing driven by collective modes in a magnetic Wigner crystal of rhombohedral hexalayer graphene.
- Showed that broken time‑reversal symmetry removes the Cooper logarithm, so a finite attraction threshold is needed for pairing.
- Hartree–Fock/TDHF calculations reveal that phonon‑mediated attraction can exceed this threshold, yielding bound electron pairs.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10710)

### Gauging Modulated Symmetries: Bond Algebras, Higher-Form Symmetries, and Symmetry-Enriched Topological Order

**Authors:** Gustavo M. Yoshitome, Pedro R. S. Gomes, Guilherme Delfino

- Constructive framework for modulated symmetries in lattice systems, using finite-dimensional representations of space group acting on finite Abelian internal symmetry.
- Kernel construction yields symmetry-allowed local interactions and an algebra of open operators; gauging maps these to extended Wilson/boundary operators preserving algebraic relations.
- In deconfined phase, the algebra realizes a mixed ’t Hooft anomaly, enabling microscopic realization of Abelian crystalline symmetry‑enriched topological phases with preserved electric‑magnetic braiding.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10764)
