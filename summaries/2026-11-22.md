### Quantum-magneto oscillations in a supramolecular Mn(II)-[3 x 3] grid

**Authors:** O. Waldmann, S. Carretta, P. Santini, R. Koch, A. G. M. Jansen, G. Amoretti, R. Caciuffo, L. Zhao, L. K. Thompson

### Ferromagnetism in Fe-doped SnO2 thin films

**Authors:** J. M. D. Coey, A. P. Douvalis, C. B. Fitzgerald, M. Venkatesan

- Fe‑doped SnO₂ thin films are transparent ferromagnets with TC ≈ 610 K and Ms ≈ 2 Am²/kg.
- Mossbauer spectra reveal high‑spin Fe³⁺; only ~23 % of Fe ions are magnetically ordered, each carrying ~8 µB.
- Ferromagnetism attributed to Fe³⁺ coupling through an electron trapped in an oxygen vacancy (F‑center).

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401293v1)

### Vortex waves and the onset of turbulence in $^3$He-B

**Authors:** K. L. Henderson, C. F. Barenghi

- Finne et al. identified the intrinsic turbulence onset criterion \(q=\alpha/(1-\alpha')<1\) in \(^{3}\)He‑B.
- The criterion corresponds to marginally damped Kelvin waves; for \(q>1\) waves cannot grow to trigger reconnections.
- Analysis of rotating‑superfluid modes confirms \(q=1\) as the crossover between damped and propagating Kelvin waves in the long‑wavelength limit.

### Field theory and the biquadratic spin-1 chain

**Authors:** Luisa Eck, Paul Fendley

- Antiferromagnetic biquadratic spin‑1 chain: gapped, SU(symmetric, integrable; low‑energy physics expected to be the CP² sigma model at θ=π.
- Bethe ansatz reveals deconfined domain‑wall excitations, matching the sigma model, yet the lattice model remains integrable while the continuum theory is not.
- Authors engineer an integrable, non‑Lorentz invariant field theory by adding a higher conserved charge and tuning to criticality; the gap closes quadratically, yielding a nonrelativistic two‑particle S‑matrix.

### Phase diagram of the attractive lattice Fermi polaron and the instability of Nagaoka ferromagnetism

**Authors:** Gerard Pascual, Kris Van Houcke

- η dimer boundary gives a rigorous, sharper instability criterion for Nagaoka ferromagnetism, logarithmic at low filling.

### Local-channel inequivalence within analytic symmetry-broken phases

**Authors:** Guanxi Xun, Rintaro Masaoka, Weiguang Cao, Haruki Watanabe

- Gibbs states at distinct temperatures in a symmetry‑broken phase are inequivalent under two‑way quasi‑local channels.
- Mutual information between distant observers grows as \( \tfrac12\ln R + \mathcal{C}(T)\), with \(\mathcal{C}(T)=\tfrac12\ln[\Upsilon(T)/T]+c_{\text{geom}}\) increasing as temperature decreases.
- The constant \(\mathcal{C}(T)\) is invariant under quasi‑local conversions, so cooling between temperatures requires non‑local operations whose causal radius scales linearly with system size.
